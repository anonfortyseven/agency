# 06 — Video, Film, Broadcast & Existing 3-D / Visual Assets — Table Rock Dam

**Subject:** Table Rock Dam, White River, Branson MO (USACE Little Rock District; built 1954–1958; concrete gravity + earthen embankment; 10-gate spillway; 4-unit powerhouse; auxiliary spillway 2005; Dewey Short Visitor Center 2012)
**Dimension:** video, film, broadcast news, stock footage, existing 3-D / physical models, webcams, 360°/VR
**Compiled:** 2026-09-05 (research agent 6 of 8)
**Purpose:** reference footage for a highly detailed 3-D model (photogrammetry-style frame extraction, vantage coverage, gate/hoist detail, powerhouse massing, auxiliary spillway, visitor center, tailwater).

---

## 0. Method, coverage and confidence conventions

- Search tool: WebSearch only (WebFetch / curl to ordinary sites are policy-blocked in this environment; confirmed by proxy status showing 403 CONNECT denials).
- **11 searches executed successfully**; the session-wide search budget (shared by all eight parallel agents) was exhausted after that (200/200), so 7 further queries were refused and ~45 planned queries were never run. Every unrun query is listed in §15 so the database can be completed in a follow-up session.
- **Confidence tags used throughout:**
  - **[V]** = URL returned verbatim by a search result (recorded exactly as returned; not visited — descriptions are taken from result titles/snippets, not from viewing the footage).
  - **[V-snippet]** = a fact stated in the search engine's result snippet/summary (not independently confirmed).
  - **[U]** = lead from training knowledge / inference — **unverified**; no URL is given unless it is a generic platform search URL, which is itself marked *(unverified)*.
- Never treat a [U] item as confirmed. Never treat a [V] title as proof of content (e.g. a "Short" titled "All 10 Gates Open" dated 2023 may be re-uploaded 2017 footage).
- Rights shorthand: **PD-Gov** = US Government work (USACE/DVIDS), public domain; **Std-YT** = standard YouTube licence (private reference use only; no redistribution); **Comm** = commercial/broadcast copyright; **RF** = royalty-free stock licence (purchase required); **UGC** = user-generated, creator-owned.

### 3a. Powerhouse interior — status
- **No powerhouse-interior video was returned** by the searches that ran. The Corps ended public dam tours roughly seven years before the "now-rare look inside" article was written (partnerships with private/non-profit tour operators dissolved) [V-snippet].
- **Print/photo lead [V]:** *"A now-rare look inside Table Rock Dam, no rumored 'aliens' or 'stockpiled munitions'"* — syndicated at https://sports.yahoo.com/now-rare-look-inside-table-103035220.html and https://www.aol.com/news/now-rare-look-inside-table-103035220.html . Snippet confirms the powerhouse houses **four generators** and describes control-room operations. Original outlet is almost certainly the Springfield News-Leader (Gannett syndication pattern) [U]; the original will carry the photo gallery (generator floor, control room, gallery corridors) — the single best interior reference found.
- [U] USACE Little Rock District has posted powerhouse maintenance/generator-rewind photos on Facebook for other White River dams; a Table Rock unit-overhaul post may exist — query in §15.

### 3b. Hwy 165 crest drive-across
- [U] Dash-cam "drive across Table Rock Dam" clips exist on YouTube from Branson road-trip channels (State Highway 165 runs along the crest; the road is the dam's crest roadway with the spillway gate structure on the lake side). Not returned in the executed searches; query in §15. Google Street View (see §10) is the reliable substitute.

## 7. Historic film

**Nothing historic was returned before the budget ran out**; the items below are research leads from training knowledge, each flagged [U]. They are organised by where the footage most likely lives.

### 7a. Construction era (1954–1958) and dedication (1959)
- **USACE construction film(s), Little Rock District** [U] — The District documented construction with 16 mm progress films and thousands of progress photographs (concrete placement from the cableway/trestle, powerhouse erection, penstock installation, embankment placement). Progress-photo sets are typically held at the Little Rock District office and the National Archives (RG 77, Records of the Office of the Chief of Engineers). Check: USACE Digital Library, NARA catalog (catalog.archives.gov, search "Table Rock Dam" in RG 77 and RG 111 motion pictures), and the Dewey Short Visitor Center theater film.
- **Dewey Short Visitor Center theater film** [U] — the visitor center runs an orientation film on the White River basin and the dam's construction; the caller's lead title *"Taming of the Riviera Blanche"* was not verified by any search and should be checked against the visitor center's own listing. Cinematography of the film includes construction footage that would be ideal for period massing of the cofferdams and the concrete non-overflow section.
- **1959 dedication (June 14, 1959; Dewey Short honoured)** [U] — Universal Newsreel and Hearst Metrotone are the two newsreel series most likely to have a story; Universal Newsreels are public domain and hosted on archive.org (collection "universal_newsreels"). British Pathé coverage is unlikely for a domestic US dam but cheap to check. Missouri State University's KOZK/Ozarks Public Television and the Springfield-Greene County Library District ("Ozarks History"/Local History) hold regional film.
- **Spring 1957 "premature fill"** [U] — heavy rains partially filled the reservoir before completion, an event covered in Springfield/Branson newspapers; any footage would be in USACE progress films or home movies. Verify the exact event before citing.
- **Home-movie transfers (8 mm/16 mm)** [U] — YouTube uploads titled "Table Rock Dam construction 1956/1957 8mm" occasionally surface from family archives; query in §15.

### 7b. Documentary and public television
- **OzarksWatch Video Magazine (Ozarks Public Television, MSU)** [U] — long-running series; the caller's lead episode *"Table Rock Lake: Beneath Still Waters"* (sunken towns/Oasis, dam history) should be searched on optv.org and the OzarksWatch YouTube channel; episodes often incorporate USACE construction footage and vintage stills.
- **Missouri Department of Conservation films** [U] — MDC video on the Shepherd of the Hills Fish Hatchery (directly below the dam) and Lake Taneycomo trout; MDC's YouTube channel; the hatchery's outdoor raceways and the tailwater intake are visible.
- **KY3 / KOLR retrospectives** [U] — anniversary pieces (50th in 2008/2009; 60th in 2018/2019) typically splice archival construction footage.
- **archive.org** [U] — generic search: *(unverified)* https://archive.org/search?query=%22Table+Rock+Dam%22 . Look in Prelinger, Universal Newsreel, and "USACE" uploads.

### 7c. Where historic *stills* (for texturing and period massing) tend to be
- Springfield-Greene County Library "Ozarks Postcards"/"Local History" digital collections; Missouri State University Libraries digital collections; State Historical Society of Missouri; Branson Centennial Museum; Table Rock Lake Historical Society (Kimberling City) — all [U]; belong primarily to the photo/archives agent but are listed here because several hold film reels as well.

Report number not recalled with confidence — do not cite one without confirming.
