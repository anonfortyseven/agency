# Table Rock Dam — Photography & Image Source Catalog

**Research dimension:** Photography and image sources (historic and modern; aerial, ground, interior, construction)
**Subject:** Table Rock Dam, White River mile 528.8, ~8 mi SW of Branson, Taney/Stone County, Missouri. USACE Little Rock District. Built 1954–1958 (concrete section completed August 1957; power on line 14 June 1959). Concrete gravity section + earthen embankments; 10-gate tainter spillway; 4-unit powerhouse (4 × ~50 MW Francis units); auxiliary spillway (8 additional crest gates) completed 2005; Dewey Short Visitor Center (2012); Missouri Hwy 165 crosses the crest.
**Purpose:** Feed an exhaustive reference database for a highly detailed 3-D model. Photographic reference from every angle and era is the priority.
**Compiled:** 2026-09-05

---

## 0. Method, constraints, and confidence conventions

- **Tooling:** Only WebSearch was usable (WebFetch/curl blocked by proxy). 50 distinct WebSearch queries were executed before the session-wide search budget (200 calls shared across eight parallel agents) was exhausted; 16 further planned queries could not be run and are listed in §7 as **NOT RUN** so they can be executed later.
- **URL policy:** Every URL below was returned verbatim by a search result unless marked **(unverified)**, which means it was inferred (e.g., from a file name seen inside a category listing) and must be confirmed before use.
- **Descriptions of image content** come from result titles, captions, and result summaries. Where the vantage was not stated in the result, it is labeled "vantage not confirmed" and given a best guess in parentheses.
- **Rights vocabulary:** PD = public domain (US federal work or expired); CC = Creative Commons (variant given); ARR = all rights reserved / commercial license required; UNK = unknown/not stated.
- **Flickr dates:** where only a Flickr photo ID is known, the upload date is estimated from the ID range and marked "≈".

## 5. Practical acquisition notes

- **PD-first workflow:** Items 1–13, 39–40 (USACE, DVIDS, Commons, USGS) can be downloaded and used without licensing. USACE igphoto pages normally expose a "Download" link to the original JPEG; DVIDS provides original-resolution video downloads (frame-grab with ffmpeg).
- **Library items (SGCL, SHSMO, MO State Archives):** research use is free; request high-resolution scans and publication permission by email, citing the item ID in the URL. Ask SGCL staff to run a "Table Rock" keyword search across the *From the Darkroom* negatives — expect many more dated frames than the four indexed here.
- **Postcards:** purchase inexpensive cards (typically $3–9) to scan at 1200 dpi; chrome cards from 1958–1977 without a copyright notice are PD in the US, but confirm publisher/notice on each card.
- **Press/stock:** use watermarked previews for proportion checks; license only frames needed for textures.
- **Commissioning:** a Branson helicopter operator (Chopper Charter) or a Part 107 drone operator (Drone Flight Squad / SVLMedia) can supply systematic orbit photography of all faces — the fastest way to close the "close-range pier/bridge/powerhouse façade" gaps. Note the dam is USACE property; drone flight over the structure requires coordination with the Little Rock District.
- **Interior:** the Corps has offered press tours (2022); a formal request to Little Rock District Public Affairs for a photo-documentation visit of the generator hall and gate galleries is the only realistic route to interior reference.

## 6. Facts gleaned from captions (useful for cross-checking other agents' dimensions)

- Dam length "more than 6,000 ft" (USACE caption) / 6,423 ft (search summary); height 252 ft above riverbed.
- Concrete volume >1,230,000 cu yd; concrete section completed August 1957; power on line 2:45 p.m., 14 June 1959.
- Spillway: 10 gates; 2019 all-gates-1-ft release ≈ 10,500 cfs spill + ~9,500 cfs hydropower ≈ 20,000 cfs; 30 May 2020 gates 3, 5, 7, 9 at 1 ft ≈ 5,500 cfs (17,500 cfs total with generation); 9 Jun 2025 five gates at 1 ft ≈ 6,500 cfs + 9,000 cfs turbines.
- Lake elevation 929.59 ft on 9 Jun 2020; top of flood pool 931 ft.
- Auxiliary spillway: 8 additional crest gates; completed 2005; ≈ $65 M; Kiewit Phase 1 excavation 254,000 cu yd.
- Powerhouse: 4 Francis turbines (Andritz, 50 MW nameplate each), 4 Westinghouse generators (52.63 MVA each).
- Records: 2008 and 2011 floods set record lake level and record spillway releases.
- Dewey Short Visitor Center address: 4500 State Hwy 165, Branson.
