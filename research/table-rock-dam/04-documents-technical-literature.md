# Table Rock Dam — Research Database
## Dimension 04: Documents, Reports & Technical Literature

**Project:** Table Rock Dam, White River, Branson (Taney/Stone County), Missouri, USA
**Owner/operator:** U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Little Rock District (CESWL), Southwestern Division
**Built:** Oct 1954 – 1958 (power on line 1959); auxiliary spillway added 2001–2005
**Structure type:** Concrete gravity (non-overflow + gated ogee spillway) + rolled earth/rockfill embankment; 4-unit powerhouse; 10-gate main spillway; 8-gate auxiliary spillway (2005)
**Compiled:** 2026-09-05 by research agent 4 of 8 (documents/literature dimension)
**Purpose of this file:** feed a highly detailed 3-D model — drawings, plans, sections, elevation tables and site maps are ranked highest.

### 11.3 How to frame the request (practical notes)

- **Security screening is certain.** Since 2001, detailed dam drawings are reviewed as Critical Infrastructure/CUI and are frequently withheld under FOIA Exemption 7(F) (and Exemption 3 via 6 U.S.C. § 673 for PCII). Improve odds by: (a) asking for *historic* (1950s) sheets and *reduced-scale general* sheets rather than structural detail/rebar sheets; (b) citing the intended use (educational/visual 3-D model); (c) accepting redaction of security-sensitive features (gate controls, galleries, instrumentation); (d) asking for the *drawing index* first so a narrow follow-up request can be written.
- **Cite precedent of release** in the letter: the Corps already publishes Table Rock Lake maps and drawings in its Digital Library (p16021coll10/id/10699 and the M-series plats), the Master Plan appendix listing the Design Memoranda (p16021coll7/id/4047), the public Dam Safety Fact Sheet (2017), the Pool History sheet, and the 2020 SMP plates. WES hydraulic-model reports of the same era for other dams (e.g., Garrison) are fully open on ERDC Knowledge Core, which supports release of TR 2-504.
- **Parallel-path NARA.** Records accessioned to NARA Kansas City are governed by NARA access rules; 1950s design memoranda and construction photographs are routinely open there. Ask NARA reference staff for RG 77, Little Rock District, "Table Rock Reservoir" project files.
- **Ask ERDC Library directly** (email reference desk) for a scan of TR 2-504 if the Knowledge Core search does not show it; they routinely digitize on request.
- **Expected timelines:** USACE FOIA simple track 20 working days; complex track (drawings) often 3–9 months. NARA reproduction 4–8 weeks.

## 16. Datum and unit reconciliation notes (read before mixing sources)

- Corps project data for Table Rock (1950s design and most Corps documents) are quoted in feet on the **NGVD 1929** datum (often labelled "MSL" on original drawings) [U — typical practice; confirm on the Fact Sheet/Master Plan]. The **USGS SIM 3499 (2022)** bathymetry is explicitly on **NAVD 1988** and quotes conservation pool 915 ft and flood pool 931 ft NAVD88 [S]. If the Corps' 915/931 are NGVD29 values, the USGS statement implies either (a) the local NGVD29→NAVD88 shift is small enough to be ignored at report precision, or (b) USGS re-derived the pool elevations. Read the SIM 3499 datum paragraph (sheet 1) and the Water Control Manual before assigning a single vertical datum to the model. Use NGS VERTCON for the offset at the dam (36.60 N, 93.31 W approx. [U]).
- Horizontal datum for 1950s drawings will be the Missouri State Plane (South zone) on NAD27 [U]; USGS 2020 data are NAD83/UTM or State Plane NAD83 [U]. Plan for a datum transformation step when georeferencing scanned as-builts.
- The 2020 SMP and 2014 Master Plan each contain a "Project Data"/"Pertinent Data" table; use it as the single elevation reference and record the datum named there.
- Discharge numbers in the summaries appear both in cfs and in gallons per second (451,000 cfs ≈ 3,373,000 gal/s) [S]; keep cfs.
