## 1. What Etsy's own publications and investor commentary say is rising

### Takeaway
Etsy has not published a trend report specific to digital items, and none of the official sources found break GMS out by category. Its official 2026 trend themes are analog hobbies, journaling, heritage crafts, whimsy, Patina Blue and Washed Linen. Those themes translate directly into printable and digital products such as journal templates, stitch and needlepoint patterns, and wall art. Etsy is also routing more discovery through AI, including a Gifting Assistant and an Etsy app in ChatGPT, but agentic traffic is still below 1% of total traffic.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy named **Patina Blue** its 2026 Color of the Year and **Washed Linen** its first-ever Texture of the Year. The summary says searches for linen clothing rose more than 1,200%. — [Etsy News](https://www.etsy.com/news/etsy-announces-its-2026-color-of-the-year-a-plus-the-first-ever-texture-of-the-year)
- The official Seller Trend Report for Spring/Summer 2026 is framed around shoppers "refreshing their homes, planning weddings and celebrations, picking up new hobbies." It draws on Etsy search data and industry forecasting. — [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1473931456647)
- **"Dear Diary" trend:** searches for "traveller journal" doubled and searches for "memory journals" rose 189%. Journaling and scrapbooking are coming back, especially among Gen Z. These figures come from press coverage of the Etsy report, not the Etsy page itself. — [Stylist](https://www.stylist.co.uk/home/etsy-spring-summer-2026-trends/1067354); [Dengarden](https://dengarden.com/news/etsy-2026-spring-summer-trends)
- **"World of Whimsy" trend:** searches for beginner needlepoint kits rose 175%. In the "Soft Stitch Era" trend, searches for embroidered straw bags rose 20,000% and crochet clothing continues to climb. — [Stylist](https://www.stylist.co.uk/home/etsy-spring-summer-2026-trends/1067354); [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-explore-the-trends-shaping-spring-and-summer-2026-165859/)
- Dayna Isom Johnson's S/S '26 Trend Edit says "heritage crafts are resurging" and that "hobbies have graduated from pastime to way of life." — [Etsy News, Trend Edit S/S '26](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/news/the-etsy-trend-edit-ss-a26athe-styles-defining-the-season-made-to-feel-like-you)
- Etsy's 2026 forecast centers on "softness, soul and perfectly imperfect" style. — [VITA Daily (Dec 2025)](https://vitamagazine.com/2025/12/13/etsys-2026-trend-forecast-softness-soul-perfectly-imperfect-style/)
- An Etsy "Christmas 2026 trending aesthetic" market page points to glittery New Year PNGs, cross-stitch patterns and retro disco-ball themes. This is an algorithmic market page, not editorial content. — [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_2026_trending_aesthetic)
- **Q2 2026 results:** marketplace GMS was about $2.6B, up 7.5% YoY (7.2% currency-neutral), the third straight quarter of YoY growth. Revenue was $668M (+6.2% continuing ops; +9.3% for the Etsy marketplace alone). Q3 GMS guidance is $2.53–2.58B (+4–6%). — [SEC 8-K Q2 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm); [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/etsy-inc-etsy-q2-2026_3PiVl8an); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/etsy-q2-earnings-call-highlights)
- **Corporate changes:** Etsy sold Depop to eBay for about $1.4B, cut about 12% of its workforce and authorized a $2B buyback. — [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/etsy-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-08/)
- **AI and gifting:** Etsy launched a beta **Gifting Assistant**, a conversational AI for gift discovery. CEO Kruti Patel Goyal said agentic traffic is "still less than 1% of our overall traffic" but carries higher intent and higher AOV. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/etsy-etsy-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/etsy-three-way-ai-bet-starting-pay-off/)
- The Q1 2026 shareholder letter says Etsy built an **Etsy App in ChatGPT**, "aligned with Open AI's shift for agentic shopping to be focused on retailer-run apps." — [SEC 8-K Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000042/q126shareholderletter.htm)
- **AI policy:** Etsy's Creativity Standards allow digital products made with AI assistance, but the seller must disclose it and must remain the creator. A purchased commercial license does not make a design "original." Sources disagree on when enforcement tightened: one blog says Jan 14, 2026, with about 12,000 listings removed in Q1 2026 (unverified); another says June 10, 2025; a third says disclosure has been required since the 2024 Creativity Standards. — [PromptlessPress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads); [ngini](https://ngini.com/en-us/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026-explained); [bulkmockup](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/). The official policy is at etsy.com/legal/creativity.

### Inferences
- Etsy's analog-hobby themes (journaling, needlepoint, cross-stitch, scrapbooking) each have a matching digital product that AI can help make: printable journal pages and junk-journal kits, cross-stitch and needlepoint pattern PDFs, and scrapbook ephemera PNGs.
- Patina Blue and Washed Linen can be used as palette and texture cues for wall art, planners and templates.
- Gift-intent AI discovery (Gifting Assistant, ChatGPT app) favors listings with clear, natural-language titles and descriptions focused on who the gift is for. "Instant gift" digital products are well placed for last-minute Q4 gifting.
- An AI-assisted shop needs AI disclosure and real creative input, because originality enforcement is a live risk.

### Gaps
- No official Etsy Fall/Holiday 2026 trend report or Trend of the Year was found. Etsy did not break out digital-download GMS in the earnings summaries.
- The full Q2 shareholder letter could not be fetched, so any digital-category commentary in it is unverified.

## 2. What seller-tool data sources say is hot, with numbers

### Takeaway
eRank's own keyword data shows "digital products" rising sharply as a buyer search term: #45 in Q2 2025, #6 in Q2 2026, #3 in June 2026 and #3 in September 2026. Competition is enormous, though, with about 16M US listings using "digital." Other tools and blogs (InsightAgent, SellerApp, Outfy, Printify) agree on the core categories: planners (especially ADHD planners), Canva templates, wall art, clipart and SVG bundles, wedding stationery, budget trackers, coloring pages and AI prompt packs. Their growth percentages are mostly unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- **eRank Q2 2026 (US):** "digital products" ranks **#6** in the Top 20 US Etsy keywords, up from **#45 in Q2 2025**. "pokemon" had the highest Q2 CTR in the Top 20 at 137%, but it is IP-protected. — [eRank Q2 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q2-2026/)
- **eRank June 2026 (US):** "digital products" is **#3** and "digital" is #19. One snippet says eRank called it a "unicorn," with 15 of 15 months above 1,000 searches and a 150% CTR in Canada. A second snippet from the same eRank posts says it **fails eRank's unicorn test because competition is heavy**: nearly **16 million** US listings use "digital." These snippets conflict. Treat "digital products" as high demand and very high competition. eRank's unicorn bar is a 125%+ current CTR, 100%+ average CTR, 5+ of 15 months with 1,000+ monthly searches, and low competition. Niche unicorns it named include "vtuber model" and "keycaps." — [eRank June 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-june-2026/); [eRank Feb 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-february-2026/)
- **eRank UK, May 2026:** "digital" had a 150% CTR. Very high CTR was also flagged for "digital planner" (#119), "digital wedding invitations" (#120) and "digital product" (#241). — eRank UK top keywords ([help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-latest-updates/](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-latest-updates/))
- **eRank Sept 2026 (US):** the Top 20 is led by **halloween, shirt, digital products (#3), christmas (#4)**. In Q3, "halloween" lost the #1 spot to "shirt" for the first time. "christmas ornament" rose from #114 to #20. "digital" and "digital planner" sit around #26–#28. — [eRank Top Keywords Now](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy/)
- **eRank Aug 2026:** "digital products" is #20 in the UK and **#10 in Australia**, where "coloring book" also makes the AU Top 20. — [eRank UK](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-latest-updates/); [eRank AU](https://help.erank.com/blog/australias-top-etsy-keywords-latest-updates/)
- In February 2026, eRank pushed back on "gloom & doomers" who call digital products too saturated. — [eRank Feb 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-february-2026/)
- **InsightAgent (vendor; data source not given):** it says AI prompt cards, ADHD planners, wedding invitation suites, budget trackers, affirmation cards and homeschool packs show "40–200%+ search volume growth YoY." It lists AI prompt cards at $4–12, rated "Very Hot" with medium competition. It calls Canva templates the fastest-growing digital segment (single template $5; sweet spot $8–14), says generic undated planners are saturated, and lists ADHD tools and financial workbooks as trending upward. — [InsightAgent trending printables](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/trending-printables-on-etsy); [InsightAgent trend tools](https://www.insightagent.app/tools/etsy-best-selling-digital-products-trend-tools-2026); [InsightAgent digital products](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)
- **SellerApp's 2026 guide (about late Sept 2026)** names these themed trends: **Nonna Holiday** (Italian printable recipe cards, kitchen wall art, Italian party supplies), **Nutcrackercore** (ballet-themed party supplies, pastel holiday wall art, editable invitation templates; "1000% YoY growth in related searches"), **Gothmas**, **Play Haus** and **Supper Club**. It says Halloween keywords started trending in July 2026. These are single-vendor figures. — [SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/)
- Printify names wedding invitations, planners, wall art and custom printables as the best digital performers. — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/)
- Several guides say editable templates (Canva/Corjl) are outselling static PDFs. They also say Canva social templates sell well to small businesses and coaches. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/digital-items-to-sell-on-etsy); [litcommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Clipart and SVG bundles remain top sellers because crafters and POD sellers keep buying them. Narrow niches like pet trackers and wedding planners convert better than generic designs. Revenue claims such as "$3k–10k/month" are unsourced. — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/); [digitalbizplr](https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/best-digital-products-sell-etsy-2026)
- **Tool accuracy caveat:** EverBee claims about 80% estimate accuracy, and Alura says its data is "a reliable indicator rather than exact numbers." Neither tool has Etsy's internal sales data. — [revenuegeeks EverBee review](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/everbee); [tagrankr](https://tagrankr.com/everbee-review/)
- **ADHD planner market check:** Etsy listings for 2026–2027 ADHD planners range from about $2.37 to $16.69 and are commonly discounted 35–75%, which suggests a crowded, price-competitive segment. — [Etsy market: ADHD digital planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/adhd_digital_planner); [Etsy listing example](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1896418231/adhd-digital-planner-adult-adhd-planner)

### Inferences
- Buyers are now literally searching for "digital products," which suggests the category is growing as a destination on Etsy. Winning listings, however, will target long-tail sub-niches rather than broad terms.
- ADHD planners have strong demand but are crowded and heavily discounted. The better opening is sub-specializing, for example ADHD budget, ADHD meal planning, ADHD for students or for parents.

### Gaps
- EverBee and Alura published no 2026 niche revenue tables that could be found. Marmalead, Sale Samurai and EtsyHunt 2026 data were not found or not accessible. YouTube and Reddit were not reviewed for lack of fetch access.

## 3. External trend signals (Pinterest, Google, TikTok, KDP, marketplaces)

Pinterest Predicts 2026 (released Dec 2025) points to nostalgia, Poetcore, Afrohemian decor, Cool Blue, gothic or "vamp" romance and 80s glam. All of these can become aesthetics for AI-generated wall art, clipart and templates. Coloring books remain big but are moving toward "bold and easy" designs and narrow niches.

## 5. High demand with relatively low listing counts (search-to-listing ratio)

### Takeaway
No verifiable keyword-level ratios of monthly searches to listing counts were obtainable. The fetches were blocked and the tools that hold this data sit behind logins. Secondary guides agree on a framework for judging competition and on a shortlist of candidate long-tail niches.

### Cited Findings
- **Framework:** search-to-listing ratio (SLR) is average monthly searches divided by active listings, with SLR above 0.5 considered "healthy" for new sellers. Suggested targets are 500–5,000 monthly searches and 2,500–50,000 listings, or under 5,000 listings for the exact keyword. — [getbetterlisting](https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/2026-etsy-niche-research-tips); [InsightAgent low-competition](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-digital-products-etsy)
- **Candidate low-competition niches named in guides (no hard numbers):**
  - specialty health trackers
  - professional-service templates such as therapist intake forms
  - pet care planners (rated medium demand, low competition)
  - niche coloring pages
  - educational posters
  - narrow clipart themes such as "watercolor western sunflower clipart" rather than generic floral
  - Source: [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-etsy-niches); [mydesigns.io](https://mydesigns.io/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/); [kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/7-untapped-digital-product-niches); [elistit](https://elistit.com/guides/etsy-digital-product-ideas/)
- Broad printable wall art and generic undated planners are described as among the most saturated. — [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/tools/etsy-best-selling-digital-products-trend-tools-2026); [getbetterlisting](https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/2026-etsy-niche-research-tips)
- **eRank unicorns** (high CTR, steady demand, low competition) named in 2026 posts include "vtuber model" and "keycaps." VTuber models are a digital, AI-adjacent product. — [eRank June 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-june-2026/)
- **High-CTR digital terms (UK, May 2026):** digital planner, digital wedding invitations, digital product. — [eRank UK](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-latest-updates/)

### Inferences
- These are the most promising candidates for checking in eRank or EverBee:
  - **Trend-aligned seasonal aesthetics:** Nutcrackercore and Gothmas printables and invitations, Nonna Holiday recipe-card kits
  - **Analog-hobby digital patterns** tied to Etsy's own trend data: needlepoint and cross-stitch PDFs, junk or memory-journal kits
  - **Professional templates:** therapist and coach intake forms
  - **Pet-care planners**
  - **Bold-and-easy themed coloring pages**
  - **VTuber assets**

### Gaps
- No verified search counts or listing counts per keyword. A follow-up with logged-in access to eRank Keyword Tool or EverBee would be needed to compute real search-to-listing ratios.
