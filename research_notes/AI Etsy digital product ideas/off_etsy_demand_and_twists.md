# Off-Etsy Demand Signals and Product Twists (2025-2026) for AI-Assisted Etsy Digital Products

Research date: 2026-10-11. Method note: ~17 web searches; direct page fetches failed for several domains (axios.com, ypulse.com returned DNS errors), so many findings rely on search-result summaries of the cited pages. Source quality is flagged inline. Many "2026 guide" sources for Gumroad/Notion/KDP are vendor SEO content — treat numbers as directional.

## 1. Which aesthetics, subcultures and hobbies are surging in 2025-2026, and what digital assets do those communities buy?

### Takeaway
The dominant 2026 macro-signal is "going analog" (craft, journaling, film, print books, dumbphones) driven by AI/digital fatigue, plus social "third place" hobbies (mahjong clubs, birding, book clubs) and maximalist/nostalgic aesthetics (Pinterest: '80s, alien/cosmic, circus, Neo Deco, "Throwback Kid"). TikTok's own 2026 forecast favors authenticity ("Reali-TEA") and "Emotional ROI" over polished fantasy aesthetics.

### Cited Findings
**Analog / anti-AI revival**
- YPulse survey: 55% of teens (13-17) and 75% of young adults (18-24) say they want to go more analog this year and take up more offline hobbies; "analog" includes reading physical books, scrapbooking, cooking, crafting, walks without headphones — [YPulse Explains: Why Gen Z is "Going Analog" (May 2026)](https://www.ypulse.com/article/2026/05/05/ypulse-explains-why-gen-z-is-going-analog/); YPulse also published a full "Going Analog Trend Report" (July 2026) — [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/report/2026/07/09/going-analog-trend-report/)
- Michaels' 2026 Creativity Trend Report (as cited): online searches for embroidery, knitting and crochet rose 136% in six months; Michaels attributes crafting to a mental-health break from doomscrolling — [La Voce di New York, Sept 2026](https://lavocedinewyork.com/en/lifestyles/2026/09/23/gen-z-turns-to-knitting-crochet-and-embroidery-to-escape-the-algorithm/)
- Searches for yarn kits ("grandma hobbies") reportedly rose 1,200% in 2025 — [The Goan, July 2026](https://www.thegoan.net/life-sunday/why-grandma-hobbies-are-winning-over-gen-z/151098.html) (secondary; figure not independently verified)
- Analog hobby searches "in some cases" up 919% in 2026, framed as AI burnout among Gen Z — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/windows/analog-revival-microsoft-windows) (figures conflict across sources; not comparable windows)
- "Tired of AI, people are committing to the analog lifestyle in 2026" — [UC Riverside School of Education, Jan 2026](https://education.ucr.edu/news/2026/01/22/tired-ai-people-are-committing-analog-lifestyle-2026)

**Dumbphones / digital detox**
- Clearpay: 24% increase in volume of "dumbphone" sales in the 12 months to June 12, 2026 vs prior year; 62% of UK parents whose child has a smartphone have considered tech to limit social media/internet access (survey of ~2,000 parents of 11-17-year-olds) — [Irish News / PA](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/24-annual-jump-in-sales-of-basic-phones-recorded-by-buy-now-pay-later-provider-MAQJ26W2Q5M7VFG3IGQUOVNEXY/)
- Dumbphone sales up 25% in 2025 per Statista (as reported) — [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/infotechnology/gadgets/celulares-minimalistas-la-tendencia-que-triunfa-contra-la-adiccion-digital-y-las-distracciones/)

**Social / "granny" hobbies**
- Yelp: searches for mahjong clubs up >4,400% YoY nationally; >40% of young adults have played; a Philadelphia club's weekly meetups grew from ~20-25 to 50-55 people — [6abc/WPVI, Aug 2026](https://6abc.com/post/mahjong-clubs-see-surge-popularity-among-younger-adults/19636787/)
- SF mahjong events with up to 30 tables / 200 guests, DJs, cocktails, Gen Z/millennial crowd — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/chinese-culture/article/3324859/how-mahjong-events-san-francisco-are-trending-gen-z-and-millennials)
- Axios Kansas City (Aug 2026) frames hobby clubs (mahjong, chess, birding) as a response to loneliness — [Axios KC](https://www.axios.com/local/kansas-city/2026/08/25/kansas-city-hobby-clubs-loneliness-mahjong-chess-birding)
- Birding described as Gen Z's latest "granny hobby," with young people starting their own clubs — [YPulse, Aug 2025](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/08/01/young-peoples-latest-granny-hobby-bird-watching/) (2025 source; no 2026 national stats found)
- Romantasy book clubs are now standard bookstore/library programming (e.g., monthly romantasy club at UK shop Lost in the Reads with Oct 2026 dates; US library "Romantasy All the Way" event Oct 18, 2026) — [Ticket Tailor / Lost in the Reads](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/lostinthereads/2268516); [Cumberland County Libraries](https://plan.cumberlandcountylibraries.org/event/17027956)
- Labubu (Pop Mart) remained a vehicle for licensed drops in 2026, e.g., a FIFA World Cup 2026 collection — [Goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/labubu-fifa-world-cup-2026-collection/blta63d5d7553852cfd)

**Platform trend forecasts**
- Pinterest Predicts 2026 (released Dec 2025; data Sept 2024-Aug 2025): 21 trends, 67% Gen Z-driven; includes "Cabbage Crush" (cabbage searches +110%), "Glitchy Glam" and "Cool Blue" beauty, home trends Afrohemian, Chapithome (circus), Neo Deco, Ovniprésent (cosmic/alien, opalescent), '80s revival, and kids' "Throwback Kid" vintage nostalgia — [Let's Data Science summary](https://letsdatascience.com/news/pinterest-predicts-21-trends-for-2026-62ab5d9c); [Axios](https://axios.com/2025/12/09/2026-pinterest-trend-report); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-publishes-2026-trend-predictions-report/807452/)
- Pinterest claims ~88% accuracy over six years (another outlet says ~8 in 10) — [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/pinterest-predicts-21-trends-for-2026-62ab5d9c)
- TikTok Next 2026 ("Irreplaceable Instinct"): three signals — Reali-TEA (authenticity over romanticized fantasy aesthetics), Curiosity Detours (exploratory discovery), Emotional ROI (shoppers reward clear emotional justification for purchases) — [TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/ko/blog/2026-whats-next-trend-report); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260124-tiktok-trend-report)
- Euromonitor 2026: "Comfort Zone" (58% of consumers report moderate-to-extreme daily stress), "Fiercely Unfiltered" (half seek products reflecting unique identity), "Wellness is going pro" — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/article/what-are-the-top-consumer-trends-in-2026)
- Statista Consumer Trends 2026 covers nostalgia, dupes, AI in retail, wellness — [Statista](https://statista.com/study/206237/consumer-trends-2026/)

### Inferences
- The analog wave creates demand for *printables that live offline*: crochet/embroidery patterns, scrapbook kits, film-photo journal layouts, dumbphone "life admin" paper systems (address books, maps, paper calendars), screen-free kid activities. Ironically, AI can be used to *produce* anti-AI/analog products, but listings should foreground hand-feel and human curation (TikTok "Reali-TEA" / Euromonitor stress-relief).
- Social hobbies (mahjong, book clubs, birding, run clubs) buy *hosting kits*: scorecards, rules cheat sheets, invitation/club branding packs, tracking logs, bingo/challenge cards, club charters.
- Pinterest aesthetics (circus/Chapithome, cosmic/Ovniprésent, Neo Deco, Throwback Kid, cabbage) are direct cues for wall art, party kits, and planner skins in late 2026.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2026 data found for run clubs, pickleball, mushroom foraging, sourdough/homesteading, or cozy gaming growth (searches returned nothing usable within budget).
- Could not access Exploding Topics' actual list or Google Trends directly; Pinterest full report not fetched (relied on summaries).
- No subreddit growth stats gathered.
- No evidence found tying romantasy readers to Labubu as a combined trend.

## 2. What new life events, pain points and regulatory changes create demand for printable/templated/custom digital help?

### Takeaway
Large, growing populations with organizing/tracking needs: 63M US family caregivers (+45% in a decade), GLP-1 users at 13.1% of US adults (with oral pills potentially expanding users ~50% by 2027), rising adult ADHD diagnoses, parents restricting kids' smartphones, and widespread under-preparedness for emergencies and for parents' aging.

### Cited Findings
- 63 million Americans (1 in 4 adults) are family caregivers, a 45% increase over the past decade (AARP/NAC Caregiving in the US 2025) — [NPA summary of AARP/NAC report, July 2025](https://www.npaonline.org/about-npa/news/news/2025/07/25/aarp-and-national-alliance-for-caregiving-release-caregiving-in-the-us)
- TIAA Institute (Feb 2026): future caregivers devote little time to concrete planning and overestimate their parents' preparedness by nearly 20 percentage points — [TIAA Institute "Unready"](https://www.tiaa.org/public/institute/publication/2026/unready-the-state-of-preparedness-of-current-and-future-caregivers)
- Only 29% of older adults say they have an emergency plan for a natural disaster (AARP survey; 2023 — older data) — [WDEF](https://www.wdef.com/?p=1435502)
- GLP-1 use rose to 13.1% of US adults in June 2026 from 12% in Nov 2025; oral GLP-1s could expand the active user base by up to 50% by 2027 (Big Chalk Analytics) — [Food Business News](https://www.foodbusinessnews.net/articles/30575-oral-glp-1s-rapidly-expanding-user-base)
- People report GLP-1s help manage some ADHD symptoms; evidence still under study (CHADD, Mar 2026) — [CHADD](https://chadd.org/adhd-in-the-news/glp-1s-for-adhd-can-medications-like-ozempic-improve-adhd-symptoms/)
- ADHD therapeutics market projected $14.67B in 2026, with rising adult diagnoses cited as a driver — [market report via KBEW](https://lifestyle.kbew98country.com/story/214036/the-market-for-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd-therapeutics-is-projected-to-us-17-85-billion-by-2030/) (press-release quality)
- 62% of UK parents of smartphone-owning kids have considered tools to limit access — [Irish News / Clearpay](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/24-annual-jump-in-sales-of-basic-phones-recorded-by-buy-now-pay-later-provider-MAQJ26W2Q5M7VFG3IGQUOVNEXY/)
- Homeschooling resources: one retail-panel source shows aggregate online sales up 20-50% in 2025 but forecasts a 20-50% decline in 2026 (narrow sample) — [GRIPS Intelligence](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/media/homeschooling-educational-resources); a vendor argues TPT's single-grade resources fit homeschool multi-age families poorly — [Chalkie.ai (biased vendor)](https://chalkie.ai/en/blog/homeschooling-resources-vs-tpt)
- 58% of consumers experience moderate-to-extreme daily stress — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/article/what-are-the-top-consumer-trends-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Caregiving is the biggest underserved "binder" market: medication logs, care-team handoff sheets, legal/financial document inventories, "parent interview" workbooks, hospital-discharge checklists. The TIAA "overestimate preparedness" finding is a natural hook for a "Have The Talk With Your Parents" kit.
- GLP-1 users need protein/fiber/hydration trackers, injection-site rotation charts, side-effect logs, small-portion meal plans, "maintenance phase" planners — a fast-growing, specific niche that KDP sources say beats generic planners.
- ADHD-adult products (body-doubling printables, dopamine menus, visual routines) overlap with GLP-1 and caregiving burden.
- Multi-age homeschool unit bundles are a positioning gap vs. TPT's grade-specific products (claim from a biased source; validate).

### Gaps
- No 2026 data found on pet ownership trends, remote-work shifts, AI-anxiety surveys, new-parent counts, or specific regulatory changes (e.g., REAL ID, school phone bans by state) within budget.
- No direct evidence of Etsy listing counts in these niches (cannot confirm "underserved on Etsy" quantitatively — should be checked with Etsy search/eRank).

## 3. What digital products are winning on Gumroad, TPT, KDP, Notion, Fiverr in 2025-2026 that are under-represented on Etsy?

### Takeaway
Off-Etsy winners skew toward high-utility, higher-priced "systems" (Notion OS templates, software/scripts, prompt packs, language guides) and toward hyper-specific planners/logbooks on KDP; Fiverr search data shows demand surging for AI video/UGC, faceless YouTube automation, and Claude Code help — services that can be productized as templates/kits.

### Cited Findings
- InsightRaider analysis of 146,271 Gumroad products: Software Development leads at $65.8M total revenue (~$60,814/product), then Business & Money, 3D Assets, Design; Writing & Publishing best entry odds (~$15,750/product across 226 products); top single earners include an AI Photoshop script (~$586K) and a $290 language guide — [InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/answers/what-digital-products-sell-best-on-gumroad) (methodology unverified)
- Gumroad perennial sellers cited: ebooks/guides, Notion templates/productivity systems, AI prompt packs; Gumroad fee 10% + $0.30 — [ConversionProPlus (SEO blog)](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/gumroad-trends-2026-what-s-selling-right-now)
- Notion: estimated top sellers Easlo ($1M+/yr), Marie Poulin (~$500K-1M/yr), Thomas Frank (>$1M template sales); prices range ~$5-15 student planners to $30-100 startup systems; sellers use Gumroad, Stan Store, Etsy, and Notion's own marketplace — [Unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates-2026); [Fungies](https://fungies.io/sell-notion-templates/) (third-party estimates)
- KDP 2026: generic gratitude journals, meal planners, weekly planners, lined notebooks saturated with thousands of titles; Amazon more actively removes low-quality/near-duplicate books; specific planners (teachers, carers, small business owners, gardening/reading/fitness hobbies) still sell; buyers reject rushed AI coloring books — [Naomi Jane Substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/low-content-books-income/)
- Profitable KDP niches: consistent year-round demand, buyers paying ~$7-20 — [WisePPC](https://wiseppc.com/blog/most-profitable-amazon-kdp-niches/); self-published titles rose 38.7% to >3.5M in 2025 (Bowker, as cited by a vendor) — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12351)
- TPT: seasonal/holiday packs and core literacy/math are backbone; Science of Reading drives literacy; digital bundles gaining — [seller blog (anecdotal)](https://www.tpt.it.com/?p=29); historically ~12 TPT sellers became millionaires, ~300 earned >$100K (older, undated) — [Education World](https://www.educationworld.com/a_news/teacherspayteachers-proves-be-huge-salary-boost-many-teachers-1084593762)
- Fiverr Business Trends Index (June 2026; searches Nov 2025-Apr 2026 vs May-Oct 2025): Claude Code specialists +938%; AI video/animation fastest-growing AI services (+278%); AI UGC video ads +265%; faceless YouTube channel automation +239%/+230%; YouTube thumbnails +52%; Instagram +147-149% vs TikTok +17-26% — [AI Magazine/GlobeNewswire](https://aimagazine.com/globenewswire/3308866); [9am.works](https://www.9am.works/freelancer-academy/blog/what-clients-ordered-2026-fiverr-malt-data); caveat that a 900% jump in a small category is still small — same source

### Inferences
- Port to Etsy: (a) "systems" not single pages — Notion/Sheets OS bundles at $20-60; (b) prompt packs bundled with a template (prompt + the doc it fills) for niche professions; (c) Fiverr-service-as-template: faceless YouTube kits (script templates, thumbnail Canva templates), Instagram carousel kits (Instagram demand outpacing TikTok on Fiverr), AI UGC ad script kits for Etsy/Shopify sellers.
- KDP's lesson (saturation + quality crackdown on AI coloring) applies to Etsy: specificity and visible quality beat volume.
- Gumroad's "language guide" outlier suggests premium, deep-niche guides (e.g., language for a specific trip/eclipse destination) can command high prices.

### Gaps
- No first-party bestseller lists for Gumroad, Notion Marketplace, Creative Market, Printables, Patreon, or Cameo were retrievable; Creative Market/Printables/Patreon/Cameo not researched within budget.
- No Etsy-side comparative listing counts gathered.

## 4. Specific cultural moments in late 2026 through 2027 worth targeting

### Takeaway
The August 2, 2027 "eclipse of the century" (Spain, Gibraltar, North Africa, Egypt; up to ~6m23s totality) already shows booking surges; plus the 2026 holiday season with analog/"Throwback Kid" themes and the ongoing mahjong/romantasy club wave.

### Cited Findings
- easyJet saw a 70% boost in bookings to totality-path destinations (Spain, Morocco, Tunisia, Gibraltar, Egypt) after the Aug 2026 eclipse; Seville/Almería/Málaga +69%, Gibraltar +29% — [easyJet](https://www.easyjet.com/en/news/airline/story/the-eclipse-effect-easyjet-sees-boost-in-bookings-to-top-spots-for-viewing-eclipse-of-the-century)
- Hotels in Cádiz reportedly paused bookings for Aug 2027; 2026 Spain eclipse est. €347.6M economic impact, ~446,000 international tourists; 2027 totality up to ~6 min 23 s in Egypt, longest land-visible until 2114 — [The Weather Network](https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/science/space/already-time-to-book-holiday-hots-pots-prepare-for-2027-solar-eclipse?1=%3D); [G Adventures](https://www.gadventures.com/blog/total-solar-eclipse-2027-where-to-see-it/)
- Romantasy book club events scheduled through Oct 2026 — [Lost in the Reads](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/lostinthereads/2372417)
- Labubu licensed drops tied to major events (FIFA World Cup 2026) — [Goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/labubu-fifa-world-cup-2026-collection/blta63d5d7553852cfd)

### Inferences
- Eclipse 2027 products: trip planners, eclipse-chasing itineraries for Spain/Egypt, kids' eclipse activity packs, photography shot-lists, personalized "I saw totality" certificates/wall art — selling window opens now through July 2027.
- Holiday 2026: "analog Christmas" (screen-free family advent, letter-writing kits), mahjong-party hosting kits for New Year, Pinterest circus/cosmic party themes.

### Gaps
- Not verified within budget (from general knowledge, needs confirmation before use): Women's FIFA World Cup 2027 (Brazil), Rugby World Cup 2027 (Australia), Super Bowl LXI (Feb 2027), US midterms (Nov 2026), LA 2028 Olympics run-up. No sourced trend data gathered for these.
