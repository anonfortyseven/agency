# Etsy Digital-Product Saturation Map and Gaps (2026)

Method note: research done 2026-10-11. Every WebFetch and curl request (to etsy.com, digitaltrends.com, craftybase.com, insightagent.app, theplrstore.com and others) was blocked by the sandbox network proxy (DNS failure or 403 on CONNECT). All findings below therefore come from WebSearch result summaries, not full-page reads. I could not pull live Etsy search-result listing counts, and the searches surfaced no r/EtsySellers threads directly. Most sources are vendor or affiliate blogs (eRank-style tools, AI-tool sellers, PLR sellers) with commercial bias. Their numbers are rough leads, not audited data.

## Q1. Per-category competition, prices, saturation signals and top-seller revenue

### Takeaway
The broad, low-effort categories are crowded: generic printable wall art, undated or blank planners, basic budget trackers, generic resumes, recolored Canva defaults, SVG cut files, coloring pages, AI prompt packs and PLR/MRR mega-bundles. The clearest sign of price compression is "mega-bundles" (millions of files for about $1). Categories that hold price ($10-$50) need setup or customization work, or serve a specific person: editable wedding suites with demos, systems built for one profession or one condition (ADHD), and comprehensive Notion "OS" templates.

**Economics**
- Etsy fees: $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, about 3% + $0.25 payment processing. Offsite Ads are charged on attributed sales within 30 days of an ad click, and all sellers are auto-enrolled; opt-out eligibility depends on trailing-365-day revenue. A $1-$3 sale loses a large share to the flat $0.25 plus $0.20. — [Outfy Offsite Ads](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-offsite-ads/); [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/custom-digital-planner)
- EverBee and similar tools show estimated gross revenue, not profit. Craftybase gives an example where a shop showing $5,000 a month nets about $800 after time, fees and subscriptions. — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/digital-products-etsy-profitability)
- Unsourced blog estimate: shops with 300-500 listings in demand categories reach $3,000-$8,000 a month. Treat as speculative. — [Busy Mom Side Hustle](https://busymomsidehustle.com/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Competition context: about 5.6M active sellers and about 95.4M buyers (Marketplace Pulse 2025, via a secondary source). — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/)
- EverBee homepage sample: a Star Map Print at about $32,087 revenue (a snapshot, not an annual figure). — [EverBee](https://www.everbee.io/)

### Inferences
- Price compression is two-tiered. "Volume" bundles (coloring pages, PLR, prompts, clipart megapacks) have collapsed to about $1 because AI makes marginal content nearly free. Products tied to an event or a workflow (wedding suites, profession or condition-specific Notion systems) hold $10-$50 because the buyer is paying for a finished outcome, not for files.
- Why the saturated categories got crowded: they once sold well because demand is huge and evergreen (home decor, organization, Cricut crafting, weddings). Those same traits plus near-zero production cost with AI drew the flood. They still sell for top sellers with review moats, deep catalogs and brand recognition.
- Buyer psychology and jobs-to-be-done:
  - Wedding and event templates: "look professional on a budget with a deadline." Demos reduce risk, and coordinated suites make larger orders easier.
  - Planners and Notion: "feel in control / fix my life", especially executive-function relief for ADHD buyers.
  - SVG: "feed my Cricut hobby with a specific theme."
  - Wall art: "decorate cheaply and instantly."
  - Business templates: "save time and look credible."

### Gaps
- No live Etsy listing counts were obtainable (proxy blocked etsy.com). The counts above are third-party tool numbers that conflict with each other.
- No verified top-seller revenue for SVG, clipart, seamless patterns, kids worksheets or digital stickers.
- No data on seamless-pattern commercial-license pricing, digital stickers (GoodNotes), or kids/teacher worksheets (likely competing with TeachersPayTeachers).
- No measured conversion-rate decline per category. One blog's 3-5% digital conversion benchmark is a heuristic. — [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/why-etsy-digital-downloads-not-selling)

## Q2. What sellers say stopped working and what still works (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
What stopped working: broad keywords, generic AI-made volume, prompt and PLR reselling, and relying on Etsy SEO alone. Sellers also report AI listings crowding search and traffic losses from Google. What still works: narrow sub-niches, Pinterest as an outside traffic source, process and use-case content on social media, and treating Etsy as a customer-acquisition channel while keeping the higher-margin back end elsewhere.

### Cited Findings
- Digital Trends (about late September 2026) reports longtime sellers quitting as AI listings crowd search. Illustrator "Viktor" (UK) reports sales down 98% in a year. Knitwear designer Chantal Miyagishima told CBC her pattern sales fell at least 50% in six months. Sellers say Etsy's AI-disclosure rule (in place since 2024) is rarely enforced. — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/)
- An Etsy community thread from mid-2025, "Etsy sales down 70% -- Can Anyone Help???", drew many replies. Its content was not readable. — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/user/viewprofilepage/user-id/14261010)
- Sellers blame personalized search ("Context Specific Ranking") for listings disappearing from search (undated, anecdotal). — [Etsy Community thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Listings-disappeared-from-search-sales-down-ads-not-working/td-p/146219938)
- One seller blog attributes its drop to Google's spam update starting in early August 2025, which cut traffic to its Etsy shops. — [CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/whay-etsy-sales-are-down)
- The February 2026 Etsy handbook change (lower visibility for US listings with shipping above $6) does not apply to digital items, which ship free. — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/etsy-search-algorithm-update-2026/)
- "Sellers who struggle compete on broad terms; those who succeed go narrow and specific." — [2026 guide summary](https://www.deadlinefunnel.com/blog/etsy-digital-products)
- Pinterest is called the #1 external traffic source for digital Etsy shops. Evergreen, keyworded pins keep driving traffic. — [Kupkaike playbook](https://kupkaike.com/sell/etsy); [Printful (older, about 2024)](https://www.printful.com/blog/how-to-use-pinterest-for-etsy)
- Hybrid model: Etsy for SEO discovery, then your own store, Gumroad or social for margin and ownership. — [Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/notion-templates-vs-etsy-digital-products-which-is-more-profitable)
- Policy changes: Etsy committed in 2024 to remove prompt-pack listings. 2025 commentary says digital downloads must be the seller's original work and PLR/MRR resale does not qualify. Many such listings remain live, which shows enforcement gaps rather than compliance. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai); [The PLR Store](https://www.theplrstore.com/what-etsys-2025-policy-changes-mean-for-plr-and-digital-sellers.html)
- AI-made items must be disclosed. — [Penluma](https://penluma.com/blog/make-money-with-ai/16-print-on-demand-etsy/)

### Inferences
- An "AI-assisted volume" shop strategy carries both market risk (price collapse) and platform risk (originality and disclosure rules could be enforced at any time).
- Shops whose products are unmistakably original and specific, and that disclose AI use honestly, have the best defensive position.

### Gaps
- No direct r/EtsySellers, r/passive_income or YouTube 2025-2026 content was retrievable. Seller sentiment comes only through press and blogs.
- The primary Etsy policy text (Creativity Standards) was not verified first-hand.

## Q3. Underserved audiences, professions, hobbies and life events

### Takeaway
The most consistent signals point to three areas. First, specific profession workflows (therapists, real-estate agents, freelance writers, fitness coaches), especially as Notion or form templates. Second, narrow sub-niches within neurodivergent support, health conditions and life stages (perimenopause, PCOS, migraine). Third, practical, data-driven pet tools, where existing pet products are mostly decorative. Senior large-print basics are already crowded. Senior caregiver-oriented and themed variants appear less served.

### Cited Findings
- Notion templates for specific professions (freelance writers, real estate agents, therapists, fitness coaches) are described as low competition with high willingness to pay. Therapist intake forms and real-estate checklists are also listed as low competition. — [Insight Agent low-competition guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-digital-products-etsy); [Insight Agent low-competition niches](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-etsy-niches)
- Any niche "that targets a specific profession, life stage, or unique workflow is still viable"; "generic productivity templates are in decline, specific-audience templates are growing." — [Kupkaike 2026](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026)
- Neurodivergent: called "one of the most under-served niches right now", though the plain "ADHD planner" is getting crowded. Specific routines and low-overwhelm sheets are suggested. ADHD planners show 10k+ reviews, which indicates demand. — [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/most-popular-digital-products-on-etsy); [Etsy market: digital products for neurodivergent](https://www.etsy.com/market/digital_products_for_neurodivergent)
- Health trackers: perimenopause, migraine trackers and PCOS meal planners are suggested. — [Insight Agent trends](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/digital-trends); [Insight Agent low-competition](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-digital-products-etsy)
- Pets: most pet digital products are aesthetic (portraits, memorials), while functional, data-driven pet management tools "barely exist". — [Insight Agent low-competition](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-digital-products-etsy)
- Hyper-specific single-person or trade products have room, e.g. a meal planner for one person, or templates for a trade few serve. — [Her Happy Habitat](https://herhappyhabitat.com/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)
- Homeschool planners are trending up. — [Insight Agent trends](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/digital-trends)
- Seniors: large-print word searches, jumbo bingo and seated exercise charts already sell in the thousands, so the basics are crowded. — [Etsy market: senior large print](https://www.etsy.com/market/senior_large_print)
- Validation heuristic (one blogger's): at least 1,200 monthly searches with under 5,000 competing listings, checked in eRank's free Keyword Tool. — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/low-competition-etsy-niches); [Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/low-competition-etsy-niches)

### Inferences (candidate gaps to validate in eRank)
- Profession systems: therapist or coach practice kits (intake, session notes, progress tracker); real-estate agent client trackers and listing checklists; trades such as electricians, landscapers and cleaners (quotes, job logs). These are bought for the outcome and hold a $15-$49 price.
- Condition-specific trackers, each narrowed further: perimenopause symptom logs, PCOS, migraine, ADHD for adults with specific contexts (college students, parents, work).
- Caregiver tools: care logs, medication schedules, family handover binders. These are adjacent to the crowded senior-activity basics but serve the adult child or caregiver buyer instead.
- Functional pet tools: puppy training logs, multi-pet health and vaccine records, foster/rescue intake.
- Languages and regions: plausible (localized Spanish, German or French wedding suites, budget sheets for UK/AU tax systems), but no evidence was found. Unverified.
- Format gap: interactive or "system" formats (Notion, Google Sheets with automation, Templett with demo) defend price better than static PDFs.

### Gaps
- No search-volume or listing-count evidence was found for Spanish or other non-English printables, regional variants, or specific trades.
- The competition scores in vendor guides (e.g. "2-4/10") have no stated methodology.

## Q4. Quality gaps: buyer complaints about low-quality AI products

### Takeaway
Documented buyer pain centers on listings whose AI mockups or images don't match the delivered product (crochet patterns are the best-documented case) and on the lack of refunds for downloads. This opens room for "verifiably real" products: tested patterns, true-to-file previews, real (not mocked) completed examples, print-tested resolution, and clear disclosure.

### Cited Findings
- NBC News: crochet pattern listings advertised with AI images led to 1-star reviews. 48 reviews on 17 listings (since August 2023) cited major discrepancies, and some buyers received instructions for a different pattern. Etsy says buyers can get a full refund via Purchase Protection when an item doesn't match its listing. (2024 article, older.) — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878)
- An analysis of 264 complaints found that 73 say Etsy is no longer "handmade" (AI and dropshipping). One cited Google Play review: "95% are AI slop and there's no refunds because it's a download." This is an anecdotal sample from app-store reviews. — [Unstar 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/is-etsy-still-handmade-ai-listings-dropshipping-reviews-2026)
- Instant-download listings typically do not accept returns, so refunds require a mismatch with the listing. — [Etsy listing policy example](https://etsy.com/listing/1007770639/lake-wall-art-lake-farmhouse-painting)
- Etsy requires listing photos to depict the product accurately. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878)
- Wedding-template friction: Templett does not work on mobile, and buyers must use a desktop. Demos cannot save edits. — [Etsy Templett listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1379346916/electronic-wedding-invitation-editable)
- PLR guidance: changing only fonts or colors is likely insufficient for originality. — [Gold City Ventures](https://goldcityventures.com/can-you-sell-plr-on-etsy-ultimate-guide/)

### Inferences
- Quality positioning that an AI-assisted but human-curated product could own:
  - (a) Tested and verified content: patterns, worksheets and trackers with accurate data and instructions.
  - (b) Previews of the actual files, not fantasy mockups.
  - (c) Mobile-friendly editable templates, addressing Templett's desktop-only limit.
  - (d) High-resolution, print-tested wall art with correct aspect-ratio sets.
  - (e) Transparent AI disclosure combined with a human-made guarantee for content.
- Likely the strongest quality gaps: pattern and instruction products (crochet, knitting, sewing), where AI fakes are most damaging, and educational worksheets, where accuracy matters.

### Gaps
- No systematic review-mining data for clipart, wall art, coloring pages or planners was obtainable. Etsy review pages could not be fetched.
- No data quantifying how AI-disclosed listings convert compared with non-AI listings.
