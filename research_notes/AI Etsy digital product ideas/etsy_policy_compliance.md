## 1. Etsy Creativity Standards and the AI policy (2024 launch, June 2025 update, 2026 claims)

### Takeaway
AI-assisted digital products are allowed on Etsy. They fall under the **"Designed by a seller"** category. The seller must **disclose AI use in the listing description**, and the design must be the seller's own original work. The verified material change is the **June 10, 2025** Creativity Standards update. It brought digital downloads into scope, removed "templated design or pattern" as an acceptable basis, and excluded bundles or scans of other people's work and AI prompt bundles. Claimed 2026 changes (a January 14, 2026 "enforcement start", a July 9, 2026 Seller Policy revision, and Q1 2026 removal counts) appear only on secondary blogs and could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy's Creativity Standards page (official) defines "Designed by a seller" as original designs that a third party produces or prints, **or that are sold as a digital download**. The same page says sellers must state in the listing description when AI was used. Its "last updated" date is June 10, and search metadata indicates 2025. — [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)
- Quoted policy text: "Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is created with the use of AI." The quote is attributed to Creativity Standards last updated June 10, 2025. — [Growtsy (quotes policy)](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy); [ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-05-08-can-you-sell-ai-art-on-etsy-2026-policy-guide)
- Etsy's Seller Handbook says Etsy allows AI-generated art where a seller "utilizes their unique creative lens and abilities, in addition to being transparent about their process." Seller-prompted AI art is the example given, such as a custom pet portrait generated from a buyer's request, classed as "Designed by a seller". — [Etsy Seller Handbook: How Etsy Is Protecting Creativity](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090)
- The official page says a bundle, scan or PDF of someone else's work does not qualify as "designed by a seller". **AI prompt bundles are excluded.** — [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)
- July 2024 launch: the Creativity Standards replaced the Handmade Policy and introduced item-detail labels ("Made by", "Designed by", "Handpicked by", "Sourced by"). TechCrunch reported that AI use must be disclosed in the description and that **AI prompts themselves cannot be sold**. — [TechCrunch, July 9, 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai)
- June 2025 change: Etsy removed the wording allowing items made "using a templated design or pattern" and now requires items to be made from the seller's original design. The same June 10, 2025 update reportedly added digital downloads to the Standards' scope and barred reselling third-party templates as your own. — [ShieldMyShop: Creativity Standards for digital downloads (Apr 2026)](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-04-11-etsy-creativity-standards-digital-downloads-template-sellers); [iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)

Production partners must be disclosed in the listing, which mainly matters to POD sellers. — [Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/); [Etsy 2026 Rules Tighten (selfemployed.com)](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/etsy-seller-ai-disclosure-fees-2026/) **[secondary]**
- **[UNVERIFIED / secondary]** "On January 14, 2026 Etsy began enforcing AI-disclosure on listings using generative AI." The same source claims about 12,000 listings removed and 8,500 warnings in Q1 2026. — [ngini.com](https://ngini.com/en-us/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026-explained); [selfemployed.com](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/etsy-seller-ai-disclosure-fees-2026/). One guide says the Creativity Standards page has **not** been revised in 2026 and that pages claiming a January 2026 enforcement date cite removal statistics with no source. — [Growtsy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy)
- **[UNVERIFIED / secondary]** Etsy is said to have revised its Seller Policy effective July 9, 2026, with "expanded options to appeal eligible listing decisions and better explanations when listings are removed or restricted". Only one source gives this date. — [news.seonib.com (July 13, 2026)](https://news.seonib.com/articles/2026-07-13/etsy-seller-rules-2026-policy-updates-fees-and-ai-guidelines.html); [bulkmockup.com](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A seller-service listing on Etsy mentions an "August 11, 2026 prohibited-items update". It is a sales pitch, not a policy source. — [Etsy listing 4533766441](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4533766441/etsy-policy-exposure-check-risk-report)
- Enforcement ladder: Etsy can remove or deactivate a listing that breaches the Creativity Standards, and can suspend the shop for repeated or large-scale violations. — [ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-05-08-can-you-sell-ai-art-on-etsy-2026-policy-guide) **[secondary]**

### Inferences
- Safe operating pattern: choose "I did" plus the "designed by" attribute, and put a plain-English sentence in the description (e.g., "This design was created by [shop] using AI image tools, then edited and assembled by hand in [tool]."). Never sell raw prompts or prompt packs. Never sell unmodified third-party templates or PLR.
- Each AI output should go through visible human selection, editing and composition. That helps under Etsy's "original design / creative lens" test, and under copyright (see section 4).

## 2. Crackdowns on digital downloads, AI spam, PLR/MRR and "commercial use" bundles; search ranking

### Takeaway
The verified crackdown lever is the **June 2025 removal of "templated design" from acceptable items**. That puts PLR/MRR resale, reskinned Canva templates and "commercial use" bundles at risk **even when the seller holds a license**. Enforcement is largely automated. Sellers report both false positives on original work and obvious template listings left untouched. Evidence of 2026 search-ranking changes aimed specifically at digital items is thin and comes only from secondary sources.

### Cited Findings

## 3. Fees, payment reserves, Star Seller, setup fee, ads

### Takeaway
US core fees: **$0.20 listing fee** (renews every 4 months or on each multi-quantity sale), **6.5% transaction fee**, **3% + $0.25 payment processing**. That is roughly 10–12% all-in before ads. **Offsite Ads** take **15%** of attributed sales for shops under $10k/yr (opt-out possible) and **12%** for shops over $10k/yr (mandatory). Newer shops pay a **one-time setup fee**, reported as $15 (some sources give a $15–$29 range by region). Payment reserves can hit new shops, including digital-only ones.

### Cited Findings
- Listing fee $0.20, charged at creation, at renewal (4 months) and when a multi-quantity item sells. Transaction fee 6.5% of the total sale including shipping and gift wrap. Processing 3% + $0.25 per order (US). — [Craftybase 2026 fee guide](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [sellerfeecalc](https://sellerfeecalc.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026); [voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026)
- Offsite Ads fee 15% (<$10k/yr, can opt out) or 12% (≥$10k/yr, mandatory), charged only on sales from an offsite ad click. Total fees can reach 20–26%. — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [haldooracademy](https://haldooracademy.com/etsy-fees-in-2026-what-every-seller-actually-pays-with-real-examples/)
- Etsy Ads (onsite) are pay-per-click against a daily budget. Etsy Plus is optional at $10/mo and includes 15 listing credits plus $5 in ads credit. Currency conversion is 2.5%. — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/etsy/pricing)
- One-time shop setup fee: one source gives "$15–$29 one-time fee for new shops (introduced September 2024)", another gives $15. — [voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026); [sellertoolshq](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/etsy-fees-explained/) **[conflicting; verify on etsy.com/legal/fees]**
- Worked example: a $50 sale with no ads costs about $5.20 in fees (~10%). With a 15% offsite ad fee, about $12.70 (~25%). — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees)
- Etsy may apply VAT on monthly seller fees depending on the seller's location and business status. — [Etsy Fees & Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/)
- Payment account reserve: Etsy holds a percentage of each new sale "of a physical item" for a period. In 2022 most reserved funds were released within about 2 weeks. A first sale, or a sale after long inactivity, can trigger a reserve. — [Etsy Seller Handbook: Payment Account Reserve](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1177649220109)
- Reserve percentage: Etsy lowered it from 75% to 30% for most sellers after 2023 seller pushback, though some sources still cite 75%. — [Indie Sellers Guild](https://indiesellersguild.org/etsy-payment-account-reserves-what-weve-learned/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-responds-to-seller-concerns-about-payment-reserves/)
- A digital-only seller reports being placed on a reserve despite Etsy's "physical item" wording. — [Etsy Community: Payment reserve](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Payment-reserve/m-p/148383058)
- Star Seller: assessed over a rolling 3 months. Criteria are a 95%+ message response rate (within 24 hours), 95%+ on-time dispatch and tracking (physical orders only), an average rating of 4.8+, and minimum order volume. Digital-only shops remain eligible because dispatch is measured only on physical sales. Older criteria also required $300 in sales and 90 days since the first sale; whether that still applies is unclear. — [Craftybase Star Seller 2026](https://craftybase.com/blog/how-to-become-etsy-star-seller); [Etsy Community: Star Seller](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Star-Seller-Issue/td-p/142283600)

### Inferences
- Effective take rate on a $5 printable: $0.20 listing + $0.33 transaction + $0.40 processing (3% + $0.25) ≈ $0.93, about 19%. Low-priced digital items are hurt badly by the fixed $0.20 + $0.25 components. Price at $8 or more or sell bundles to dilute them.
- For a digital shop, Star Seller turns mostly on message response speed and ratings.

- No official confirmation of whether reserves currently apply to digital-only sales.

## 4. Personalized/custom orders delivered digitally (delivery, messaging, refunds, cases)

### Takeaway
Made-to-order digital files must be delivered **within 7 days of purchase**. A buyer can open a case once they have downloaded the file, or once 7 days have passed since purchase and 48 hours since a "Help with order" request. Purchase Protection covers eligible non-delivery and not-as-described cases up to $250. Sellers must publish clear custom-order and return policies. Digital downloads are generally non-returnable once downloaded, but "not as described" (e.g., wrong personalization) is still actionable.

### Cited Findings
- "Made-to-order downloads should be made available for download within 7 days of purchase." — [Etsy Cases Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/cases-policy/243306189901)
- For made-to-order digital items, a case can be opened if "You downloaded the item; or 7 days have passed since you purchased the item, and 48 hours have passed since you sent the seller a Help with order request." — [Etsy Cases Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/cases-policy/243306189901); [Etsy Help: How to Open a Case](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/5745586898199-How-to-Open-a-Case)
- Purchase Protection Program for Sellers covers eligible non-delivery, damaged and not-as-described cases, with Etsy refunding buyers up to $250 USD and sellers not held responsible. The cited version is labeled "Effective until May 6, 2026", which suggests a newer version took effect May 7, 2026. — [Etsy Purchase Protection Program for Sellers](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/purchase-protection-program-for-sellers/1478632171992)
- Sellers should fill out shop policies for returns, exchanges and custom orders. Those policies must comply with Etsy rules and local law. — [Etsy Buyer Policy / House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/legal/buyers/)
- Instant-download listings commonly state "don't accept returns, exchanges or cancellations". An accurately described file that merely disappoints a buyer reportedly isn't eligible for a not-as-described case. — [listifyai](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds) **[secondary]**; [Etsy listing example](https://etsy.com/in-en/listing/1443633464/silver-lion-high-resolution-digital)

### Inferences
- For AI-personalized items (custom portraits, named prints): collect personalization at checkout, confirm by Etsy Messages (Etsy's records support case defense), deliver a proof, then upload the final file via Etsy's "complete order" digital-file upload well inside the 7-day window. State the turnaround time and AI involvement in the listing so "not as described" claims are hard to sustain.
- Keep all communication on Etsy Messages. Off-platform delivery or contact weakens Purchase Protection eligibility. This is an inference and needs verification against the current program text.

### Gaps
- The May 2026 revision of Purchase Protection (what changed) could not be read.
- The EU 14-day withdrawal right for digital content, which a seller can waive with explicit consent at purchase, and how Etsy's checkout handles it were not researched in this pass.

## 5. IP and copyright risks (AI output copyrightability, trademarks, right of publicity, Etsy takedowns)

### Takeaway
In the US, **purely AI-generated output is not copyrightable**. Human authorship is required, and the Supreme Court denied cert in *Thaler v. Perlmutter* on **March 2, 2026**. AI-assisted works can be protected only for the human-authored expressive elements. Prompts alone are not enough, per the Copyright Office's January 2025 Part 2 report. Sellers therefore have weak legal protection against copycats while carrying full liability for infringing others' trademarks, characters and likenesses. Voice and likeness: Tennessee's ELVIS Act is in force (since July 1, 2024). The federal **NO FAKES Act of 2026** (S.4591 / H.R.8915) cleared Senate Judiciary in June 2026 but is **not law**.

### Cited Findings
- US Copyright Office, *Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability* (January 29, 2025) affirms that US copyright requires human authorship. Commentators read it as holding that prompts alone, however detailed, are insufficient, while human selection, arrangement and modification can be protected. — [Norton Rose Fulbright (2026 AI litigation update)](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/ce8eaa5f/ai-in-litigation-series-an-update-on-ai-copyright-cases-in-2026); [garrettham.com](https://garrettham.com/ai-generated-works-copyright/)
- The D.C. Circuit (March 2025) held that copyright requires human authorship. The requirement does not bar works "made by or with the assistance of artificial intelligence" if a human is the author. — [Finnegan](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/ip-updates/supreme-court-declines-to-hear-thaler-v-perlmutter-leaving-human-authorship-requirement-intact.html); [Baker Donelson](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/supreme-court-denies-certiorari-in-thaler-v-perlmutter-ai-cannot-be-an-author-under-the-copyright-act)
- The Supreme Court denied certiorari without comment on March 2, 2026 (No. 25-449). DOJ argued the case was a poor vehicle for AI-assisted authorship questions because Thaler disclaimed any human contribution. — [Holland & Knight (Mar 2026)](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/the-final-word-supreme-court-refuses-to-hear-case-on-ai-authorship); [Munck Wilson](https://www.munckwilson.com/news-insights/supreme-court-denies-certiorari-in-thaler-v-perlmutter-human-authorship-still-required/)
- AI-generated content built on protected characters is reportedly removed instantly on Etsy. — [promptlesspress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products) **[secondary]**
- Etsy IP process: rights holders or their agents report through the Etsy Reporting Portal. Etsy removes compliant-notice content and notifies the seller, who may counter-notice where applicable. — [Etsy Intellectual Property Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/ip/); [Etsy IP report form](https://www.etsy.com/legal/ip/report)
- 2024 Transparency Report: Etsy rejected 15% of IP infringement reports, a 44% drop from 2023, attributed to stricter requirements for authorized reporters. — [Etsy 2024 Transparency Report (PDF)](https://investors.etsy.com/_assets/_c3886e15e94448260b8e186be2e272e8/etsy/db/1016/9831/pdf/2024_Transparency_Report_Digital.pdf)
- The 2025 Transparency Report (covering Jan–Dec 2025) exists, but the takedown figures could not be extracted. — [Etsy 2025 Transparency Report (PDF)](https://storage.googleapis.com/etsy-custom-assets/TransparencyReport2026/2025_Transparency_Report_Digital.pdf)
- Repeated IP infringement can lead to shop suspension, and responding to notices is time-critical. — [Harper James](https://harperjames.co.uk/article/etsy-copyright-infringement/); [Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/news/business/etsy-intellectual-property-guide-for-sellers) **[secondary]**
- Tennessee ELVIS Act: signed March 21, 2024, effective July 1, 2024. It adds **voice** to protected name, photograph and likeness, and makes unauthorized commercial use, including AI imitations, actionable. It also creates liability for distributing tools whose primary purpose is unauthorized reproduction of voice or likeness. Some violations are a Class A misdemeanor. — [Armstrong Teasdale](https://www.armstrongteasdale.com/thought-leadership/artificial-intelligence-and-copyrights-tennessees-elvis-act-becomes-law/); [MSBA](https://www.msba.org/site/site/content/News-and-Publications/News/General-News/ELVIS-Act.aspx); [Vanderbilt JETLaw](https://scholarship.law.vanderbilt.edu/jetlaw/vol27/iss2/2/)
- NO FAKES Act of 2026: S.4591 was introduced May 20, 2026. Senate Judiciary advanced it unanimously in June 2026; sources give June 18 or June 22. It was placed on the Senate Legislative Calendar on June 24, 2026. The House companion, H.R.8915, is in committee. The bill would create a federal right in one's digital replica, preempt some state laws, and add a DMCA-style notice and counter-notice process. GovTrack estimates a 27% chance of enactment. — [Congress.gov S.4591](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/4591); [GovTrack](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/s4591); [Manatt](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/client-alert/congress-reintroduces-the-no-fakes-act-what-s-new-in-the-2026-bill); [Holland & Knight (June 2026)](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/senate-judiciary-committee-advances-legislation-to-protect-name); [ByteBack Law (Aug 2026)](https://www.bytebacklaw.com/2026/08/a-federal-shift-in-ai-and-the-right-of-publicity-no-fakes-act-advances-in-congress/); [Congress.gov H.R.8915](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/8915/text)

### Inferences
- Hard "no" list for an AI digital shop: brand names and logos, characters (Disney, anime, Bluey, etc.), celebrity likenesses or "in the style of [living artist]" marketing, sports team marks, and AI voice clones of real people. Use trademarked terms in neither listing titles nor tags.
- Because raw AI outputs aren't copyrightable, copycat sellers can lawfully copy them. Building in human-authored elements (original text, layout, hand edits) gives the only enforceable protection.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 Etsy IP takedown statistics broken out for digital items or AI. The 2025 PDF could not be parsed.
- Pending AI training cases (e.g., Getty v. Stability, NYT v. OpenAI, music-label suits) were not researched in depth. Their outcomes affect tool-vendor risk more than seller risk.

## 6. Tax: VAT / sales tax on digital goods

### Takeaway
Etsy acts as a **marketplace facilitator**. It calculates, collects and remits **US sales tax** on qualifying digital orders to US buyers on behalf of sellers worldwide. It also collects and remits **VAT on digital items** where required, such as for EU and UK buyers. The tax treatment depends on the listing category, so categorize correctly. Sellers keep their own income-tax obligations and any off-Etsy sales tax obligations.

### Cited Findings
- "Etsy automatically calculates, collects, and remits US sales tax on behalf of sellers located anywhere in the world when a digital order is purchased by a US buyer and the order meets certain criteria." Treatment depends on each taxing authority and on how the item is listed. — [Etsy Help: How US State Sales Tax and Fees Applies to Etsy Orders](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343968)
- One 2026 guide says Etsy collects in every US state with a statewide sales tax plus DC and Puerto Rico. Another says "most states". — [Craftybase](https://api.craftybase.com/blog/etsy-marketplace-sales-taxes); [1800Accountant](https://1800accountant.com/blog/etsy-store-sales-tax) **[secondary, conflicting detail]**
- Etsy "will automatically display the increased amount charged to the buyer of the digital item to collect the correct amount of VAT, and we will remit it to the tax authorities." Where Etsy must collect sales tax or VAT, it uses the seller's listing category to calculate it. — [Etsy Fees & Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/)
- Sellers may still need to file returns even when Etsy remits, and remain responsible for off-platform sales. Sellers should not add sales tax into the listing price. — [zamp](https://zamp.com/etsy-sales-tax); [outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-sales-tax-explained/) **[secondary]**

### Inferences
- A US-based AI digital seller selling only on Etsy generally needn't register for state sales tax for Etsy sales. They do need to report income, and Etsy issues a 1099-K above the federal threshold (not researched here).

## 7. AI tool licensing pitfalls

### Takeaway
Free tiers of the major generators generally **do not allow commercial use**. **Suno free:** non-commercial only, Suno retains rights, and later upgrading does not retroactively license free-tier songs. **Midjourney free trial:** reportedly CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial. Midjourney paid plans grant ownership "to the fullest extent possible under applicable law", but companies earning more than $1M a year need Pro or Mega. Even on paid plans, ownership wording is qualified, Midjourney gets a broad license to outputs, and there is no indemnity.

### Cited Findings
- Suno free plan: outputs are non-commercial and can't be monetized, even if the user later subscribes. Suno "retains all rights, title, and interest" in free-tier outputs, and attribution to Suno is required. — [terms.law: Suno](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/suno/); [replayedstudio](https://replayedstudio.com/blog/suno-commercial-rights-explained/); [techjacksolutions](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-tools/suno/suno-commercial-use/)
- Suno Pro (~$10/mo) and Premier (~$30/mo) give commercial rights for songs made while subscribed. — [dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/ai-music-distribution/suno-commercial-rights-explained); [rinzara](https://rinzara.com/suno-pricing)
- After Suno's November 2025 settlement and partnership with Warner Music, its terms reportedly dropped "own" language. One source says paid users are "generally not considered the owner"; others say rights are assigned. This is disputed. — [Music in Africa](https://musicinafrica.net/magazine/suno-adjusts-ai-music-ownership-terms-after-warner-music-partnership/); [TopHit](https://tophit.com/news/pro/14283); [Techpresso](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-suno-worth-it)
- UMG and Sony litigation against Suno: status is conflicting across sources as of April 2026. — [hookgenius](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-legal-guide/) **[unresolved]**
- Midjourney: companies earning more than $1M a year need a Pro or Mega plan for commercial use (one source says parent companies and affiliates count). Free-trial images reportedly carry only CC BY-NC 4.0. — [terms.law Midjourney 2026 guide (Jan 15, 2026)](https://terms.law/2026/01/15/midjourney-commercial-use-rights-complete-2026-guide/); [terms.law: Midjourney](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/midjourney/)
- Midjourney: Stealth Mode, which keeps images out of the public gallery, is only on the Pro and Mega tiers. Otherwise outputs are public and can be copied by competitors. Midjourney takes a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to user content and gives no IP warranty or indemnity. — [terms.law comparison](https://terms.law/Demand-Letters/Guides/ai-tools-commercial-rights-comparison.html); [ConductAtlas (Midjourney ToS clause)](https://conductatlas.com/platform/midjourney/midjourney-terms-of-service/perpetual-royalty-free-content-license-to-midjourney)

### Inferences
- Seller checklist: (1) generate only on a paid tier that grants commercial rights, and keep receipts and a screenshot of the ToS version in force; (2) never sell outputs made on a free tier; (3) on Midjourney, use Pro with Stealth if competitors could scrape the public gallery; (4) for music or voice products, avoid any voice resembling a real artist (ELVIS Act, pending NO FAKES).

### Gaps
- Not checked: current official ToS text for Midjourney, Suno, Udio, OpenAI (ChatGPT image or Sora), Adobe Firefly (commercially-safe and indemnified), Ideogram, Leonardo or Canva AI. All findings above come from third-party summaries, not the vendors' pages.
