# Sell Finished Experiences, Not AI Files

The best AI digital products to sell on Etsy in late 2026 are not more wall art, planners or clipart. Those markets collapsed under AI-made volume. The best products are **finished experiences and personal artifacts**, things a buyer can't make in an afternoon with a free chatbot. The strongest bets for a solo seller this Q4 are **printable murder-mystery and holiday party games**, **custom photo-to-cross-stitch patterns** alongside premade trend charts, **heirloom family recipe books** built from a buyer's handwritten cards, **custom photo coloring books**, **caregiver "have the talk" binders**, **mahjong night host kits** and **Gothmas/Nutcrackercore holiday printables**. Behind them come personalized character videos, GLP-1 journey trackers and offline interactive HTML games. Etsy demand for digital goods is real. eRank shows "digital products" rising from the #45 US search term in Q2 2025 to **#3 in September 2026**. But about **16 million US listings use "digital"**, AI flooding has cut some established sellers' sales by 50–98%, and Etsy's June 2025 Creativity Standards now put template, PLR and prompt-pack shops at policy risk. The winning pattern is narrow audience, visible human testing or curation, honest AI disclosure, and a price of at least $10–$15 so Etsy's fixed fees don't eat the margin. One limit applies to everything below. **The research could not reach etsy.com** (the proxy blocked it), so no live listing counts were collected, and every idea should pass an eRank or EverBee search-versus-listing check before you spend real production time on it.

## Etsy demand is rising while generic AI supply drowns it

The demand picture is better than the doom suggests. Etsy's marketplace GMS grew **7.5% year over year to about $2.6B in Q2 2026**, its third straight quarter of growth ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)). Buyers now type "digital products" as a destination search: **#6 in Q2 2026, up from #45 a year earlier** ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q2-2026/)), and **#3 in September 2026**, behind only "halloween" and "shirt" and ahead of "christmas" ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy/)). eRank itself warns that the broad term fails its "unicorn" test because **nearly 16 million US listings use "digital"** ([eRank June 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-june-2026/)). So demand is rising but the head terms are unwinnable, and every opportunity in this report lives in the long tail.

Supply has split into two tiers. Volume products like coloring pages, clipart, PLR and prompt packs have collapsed toward $1 "mega-bundles", such as a **"10,000,000+ coloring pages" bundle** ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/ai_coloring)). Products bought for a finished outcome still hold $10–$50. One editable wedding invitation suite at $14.99 shows about **689 sales and $10,328 revenue** on EverBee ([EverBee](https://www.everbee.io/product-analytics)). The cost of the flood is falling on sellers. Digital Trends reports a UK illustrator whose **sales fell 98% in a year** and a knitwear designer whose pattern sales fell **at least 50% in six months** as AI listings crowded search ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/)). Buyers have noticed too. One analysis of 264 app-store complaints found 73 saying Etsy is no longer "handmade", including the line "95% are AI slop and there's no refunds because it's a download" ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-etsy-still-handmade-ai-listings-dropshipping-reviews-2026)).

Two forces line up for a careful AI seller. Culture is swinging analog. **55% of teens and 75% of 18–24-year-olds** say they want to go more analog ([YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/article/2026/05/05/ypulse-explains-why-gen-z-is-going-analog/)). Michaels reports embroidery, knitting and crochet searches up **136% in six months** ([La Voce di New York](https://lavocedinewyork.com/en/lifestyles/2026/09/23/gen-z-turns-to-knitting-crochet-and-embroidery-to-escape-the-algorithm/)). Etsy's own trend report shows "memory journals" searches up **189%** and beginner needlepoint kits up **175%** ([Stylist](https://www.stylist.co.uk/home/etsy-spring-summer-2026-trends/1067354)). Meanwhile AI tools crossed real quality thresholds in 2026: native 2K images in Midjourney V8.1 ([Midjourney](https://docs.midjourney.com/docs/models)), true editable SVG from Recraft V4.1 ([IconScout](https://iconscout.com/ai-models/recraft-vector)), reliable text-heavy graphics from GPT Image and Ideogram ([xainflow](https://www.xainflow.com/es/blog/best-ai-image-generators-2026-comparison)), and music generation trained on licensed data from ElevenLabs ([gradually.ai](https://www.gradually.ai/en/elevenlabs-music/)). The opening is to **use AI to make analog, personal, tested products**, where AI does the tedious work and the human value stays visible.

The ideas below do two things. Some follow demand the research actually measured. Others take the reason a saturated category sells (gift intent, "feel in control", hobby feeding) and deliver it in a form the flood can't copy cheaply.

### 1. Murder-mystery and holiday party game kits have the best measured ratio

This is the one idea with a numeric demand-to-supply signal. "Murder mystery party" shows roughly **22,200 monthly searches against about 5,840 listings, mostly priced $10–$25** ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/murder-mystery-party)). That is a search-to-listing ratio near 3.8, far above the 0.5 "healthy" threshold sellers use ([getbetterlisting](https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/2026-etsy-niche-research-tips)). Guides also say buyers have moved from "just files" toward themed products and interactive games ([Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/most-popular-digital-products-on-etsy)). The **premade product** is a themed kit of 6–12 character booklets, clue cards, host script, invitations and a solution envelope, released in seasonal editions: Gothmas manor, 1920s New Year's Eve, Nutcracker ballet backstage. The derived **custom twist** is a "your cast" edition. The buyer submits guests' names, quirks and in-jokes, and you deliver a kit where every suspect is a real friend, with stylized character portraits. The AI workflow uses Claude or ChatGPT to draft plot, timelines and clue logic, GPT Image or Ideogram for consistent text-heavy props and character cards (2026 models handle text much better, though long text still breaks, per [Dan Taylor-Watt](https://dantaylorwatt.substack.com/p/when-ai-image-generators-can-research)), and Canva or Affinity for layout. The **moat is a playtest**: one real run-through per kit to catch logic holes AI introduces. That is also the main risk. A broken solution produces "not as described" cases, and a custom order has to clear proof and delivery inside Etsy's 7-day window.

### 2. Cross-stitch is the AI pattern craft that actually works

Etsy's own data shows needlepoint and heritage crafts surging. Its Christmas 2026 market page surfaces cross-stitch patterns ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/christmas_2026_trending_aesthetic)), and craft searches are up 136% ([La Voce di New York](https://lavocedinewyork.com/en/lifestyles/2026/09/23/gen-z-turns-to-knitting-crochet-and-embroidery-to-escape-the-algorithm/)). Crochet and knitting patterns fail because AI cannot test three-dimensional construction ([Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/11/24/the-ai-invasion-of-knitting-and-crochet/)). Cross-stitch and needlepoint are different. They are flat grids, and converting an image to a chart is mechanical work for charting software. AI only has to design the motif, so the output is reliable. The **premade line** is beginner charts in 2026 motifs: Patina Blue palettes ([Etsy News](https://www.etsy.com/news/etsy-announces-its-2026-color-of-the-year-a-plus-the-first-ever-texture-of-the-year)), gothic florals and plum noir ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/fall-2026-trends-etsy-sellers/)), cabbage and cosmic motifs from Pinterest Predicts ([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/pinterest-predicts-21-trends-for-2026-62ab5d9c)). The **derived custom twist** takes on saturated pet portraits from a new angle. Pet portraits prove enormous gift demand at $3.99, but a **"your pet / your house / your wedding venue as a cross-stitch pattern"** PDF is a hobby product and a gift at once. It sells to stitchers who want to make the gift themselves, a buyer motive the $4 portrait sellers don't serve. The workflow is an image model (Recraft or Midjourney paid tier) to simplify the photo or motif into flat color regions, charting software to generate symbol and color charts with a floss key, and an **actual stitched sample photographed for each premade design**. That photo is a direct answer to the AI-mockup complaints NBC documented ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878)). The risks are stitch-count errors (proofread the chart) and buyers sending low-resolution photos, so publish photo requirements up front.

### 3. Heirloom recipe books turn the Nonna Holiday trend into a $50–$99 custom order

Three signals converge here. SellerApp names **Nonna Holiday** (Italian printable recipe cards and kitchen art) as a Q4 2026 theme ([SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/)). Memory-journal searches are up 189% ([Stylist](https://www.stylist.co.uk/home/etsy-spring-summer-2026-trends/1067354)). Euromonitor finds half of consumers seek products that reflect their unique identity ([Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/article/what-are-the-top-consumer-trends-in-2026)). The derived product is a **custom digital cookbook**. The buyer photographs a parent's or grandparent's stained, handwritten recipe cards. A vision-capable LLM transcribes and standardizes them (units, steps, servings), AI generates watercolor chapter illustrations in a consistent family style, and you lay out a print-ready PDF that keeps scans of the original handwriting beside each typed recipe. That last part is what makes the book an heirloom rather than a template. A premade entry tier, editable Nonna-style recipe card templates at $8–12, captures cheaper traffic and feeds the upsell. Competition is likely low because the work is intake-heavy, which is exactly what deters volume sellers. The price can sit at $49 for up to 20 recipes and $99 for 50, since the buyer is paying for a family keepsake. The risks are transcription errors (always send a proof), intake friction (photos must come through Etsy Messages, so cap weekly order slots), and the 7-day delivery rule, which means a **mid-December order cutoff** for Christmas.

### 4. Custom photo coloring books have almost no digital-only sellers

A shop selling 7-photo-page coloring books charges an **$18 printable-PDF add-on, takes 7–10 business days and had only 2 reviews** ([Etsy ShopColorMeYours](https://www.etsy.com/shop/ShopColorMeYours)). Other shops in the space are physical-first ([Etsy MyWorldinColorBooks](https://www.etsy.com/mx/shop/MyWorldinColorBooks)). That points to thin digital competition at a price well above commodity coloring pages. Timing is favorable. Amazon adult-coloring search peaked in December 2025 and sales in January 2026 ([KDPEasy](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)). The personal angle also dodges buyer rejection of "rushed AI coloring books" ([Naomi Jane](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)). The product turns 10–20 family, pet or vacation photos into bold-and-easy line art, assembled with a custom cover and the child's or family's name. Bold-and-easy is the dominant coloring style, at about 40% of Amazon's top 50 ([bookillustrationai](https://bookillustrationai.com/blog/bold-and-easy-coloring-books-amazon-kdp)). The workflow is photo-to-line-art editing in GPT Image or Nano Banana Pro, line cleanup or vectorization in Recraft, and layout in Canva. Price at $19 for 10 pages and $35 for 20, with delivery in 2–3 days to beat the 7–10-day incumbents. The defining risk in AI personalization is **likeness failure**. "It does not look like me" is the top complaint about AI headshots ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/aragon-betterpics-remini-headshotpro-photoai-ai-headshot-apps-ranked-2026)), so include a proof step and one free revision. Children's photos also call for a written deletion-after-delivery policy.

### 5. The caregiver binder serves 63 million people the planner flood ignores

**63 million Americans, one in four adults, are family caregivers, up 45% in a decade** ([NPA / AARP-NAC](https://www.npaonline.org/about-npa/news/news/2025/07/25/aarp-and-national-alliance-for-caregiving-release-caregiving-in-the-us)). Future caregivers **overestimate their parents' preparedness by nearly 20 percentage points** ([TIAA Institute](https://www.tiaa.org/public/institute/publication/2026/unready-the-state-of-preparedness-of-current-and-future-caregivers)). Senior large-print activity printables are already crowded, but those are bought for the senior. This product is bought by the **adult child**, a different and underserved buyer ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/senior_large_print)). The premade kit holds a medication log, care-team handoff sheets, doctor-visit prep, a document and password inventory, an emergency grab-and-go page, and a guided **"Have the Talk" interview workbook**. The Q4 twist is timing: market it for Thanksgiving and Christmas visits, when families are in the same room. Offer fillable PDF plus Google Sheets or Notion versions at $29–39, since "system" formats defend price better than static PDFs ([Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026)). AI handles drafting the question sets, plain-language explanations and consistent icons. A human checks every medical and legal item and adds a clear "not medical or legal advice" disclaimer. The risk is advice liability, which the disclaimer and staying with organizational rather than advisory content address. Etsy-side demand also needs confirming, because the caregiver evidence comes from outside Etsy.

### 6. Mahjong host kits ride a 4,400% social-hobby spike

Yelp searches for mahjong clubs are up **more than 4,400% year over year**. One Philadelphia club grew from about 20–25 to 50–55 weekly attendees ([6abc](https://6abc.com/post/mahjong-clubs-see-surge-popularity-among-younger-adults/19636787/)), and San Francisco events draw up to 200 Gen Z and millennial guests ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/chinese-culture/article/3324859/how-mahjong-events-san-francisco-are-trending-gen-z-and-millennials)). Social hobbies buy **hosting kits**: invitations, scorecards, rules cheat sheets for each variant (Chinese, Riichi, American), a "learn in 30 minutes" guide, table signs and club charters. The derived Q4 product is a **New Year's Eve mahjong party edition**. A custom upsell, $25–45, brands a club with its name, logo, membership cards and a season scoreboard. AI produces the illustrated tile-themed art and editable Canva layouts, and rules text is human-verified. On risk, do not reproduce the annual National Mah Jongg League card, which is a copyrighted product. Write original rule summaries instead. Treat the game's Chinese heritage with care in design and copy, because restyled "aesthetic" mahjong products have drawn backlash before. Etsy listing counts are unknown, so validate first.

### 7. Gothmas and Nutcrackercore printables have a short, hot window

SellerApp reports **Nutcrackercore at "1000% YoY growth in related searches"** and names Gothmas and Nonna Holiday as 2026 holiday themes ([SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/)). eRank's fall guide lists plum noir and gothic florals ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/fall-2026-trends-etsy-sellers/)), and "christmas ornament" jumped from #114 to #20 in US search ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy/)). Editable Canva and Corjl templates now outsell static PDFs ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)), so build **editable suites**: party invitations, menus, place cards, a printable ornament set and gift tags. Price $8–15 per piece and $18–29 per suite. The AI workflow is Midjourney V8.1 or Nano Banana Pro for illustration, Recraft for SVG ornament cut files, and Canva for editable text. This idea ranks only seventh for two reasons. The 1000% figure comes from a **single vendor** with no stated method, and the window closes in late December. Treat it as fast cash flow and a traffic driver for ideas 1–3, not a long-term line. The Nutcracker story and Tchaikovsky's ballet are public domain, but specific productions' and studios' character designs are not.

### 8. Personalized character videos sit in an empty price band

A UK Etsy shop sells human-recorded Santa videos ([Etsy CustomSantaVideo](https://www.etsy.com/mx/shop/CustomSantaVideo)). App rivals reportedly charge about $1.99 through subscription ([Toolmage](https://www.toolmage.com/en/tool/santacard/)), and pandemic-era human videos sold for $33.99 ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/361361)). That leaves a $12–25 band for a **custom, AI-animated video naming the child, their pet, their wish and an accomplishment**, delivered in 48 hours. Demand for AI video creators rose 66% in six months on Fiverr ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/news/2025-fall-business-trends-index)), though that is business demand and consumer demand is inferred. Extend the line past Christmas with tooth-fairy, birthday-dragon and graduation characters. The workflow is an LLM script from the order form, an **original** character voice from ElevenLabs (never a clone of a real person; Tennessee's ELVIS Act makes AI voice imitation actionable, per [Armstrong Teasdale](https://www.armstrongteasdale.com/thought-leadership/artificial-intelligence-and-copyrights-tennessees-elvis-act-becomes-law/)), and image-to-video in Veo 3.1 (about $0.40 per second) or Kling 3.0. The biggest risk is **tool churn**. OpenAI shut the Sora app in April 2026 and the API in September, and Google retired the Veo 3 API in June ([TokenMix](https://tokenmix.ai/blog/sora-api-shutdown-alternatives-2026)). Build templates that can move to another video model. Demand also compresses into roughly three December weeks, which strains the 7-day delivery rule.

### 9. GLP-1 trackers target a 13% adult population that peaks in January

GLP-1 use reached **13.1% of US adults in June 2026**, and oral GLP-1 pills could expand the user base by up to **50% by 2027** ([Food Business News](https://www.foodbusinessnews.net/articles/30575-oral-glp-1s-rapidly-expanding-user-base)). KDP sellers report that specific planners still sell where generic ones are saturated ([Naomi Jane](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)). The premade kit tracks protein, fiber and hydration, includes an injection-site rotation chart (with a separate pill-schedule version), a side-effect log, small-portion meal templates and a maintenance-phase plan, at $9–17. The derived custom upgrade is a **personalized protein meal grid** generated by an LLM from the buyer's food preferences and restrictions, at $19–29. The launch window is mid-December for New Year's resolution traffic. The main risks are **drug brand names**: keep Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound and similar marks out of titles and tags, consistent with the trademark "hard no" list in the compliance section. Use generic "GLP-1" language and medical disclaimers, and frame the meal grid as a planning tool, not dietary advice.

### 10. Offline HTML apps are the experimental, newly possible format

Coding assistants can now produce working single-file web apps, and Cursor, Lovable and Bolt terms assign generated code to the user for commercial use ([ExitBid](https://exitbid.io/blog/sell-vibe-coded-app)). That makes a new kind of "digital download" possible: an **.html file that runs offline in any browser**. Examples are a digital escape room that checks lock codes, a wedding budget calculator, or a kids' quiz game with the child's name. Etsy already lists vibe-coding guides, but demand evidence is for instructional PDFs, not the apps themselves ([Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/it/market/base44_app)). This is **pure derivation with no measured demand**. List one or two escape-room titles as companions to idea #1 and watch conversion. Price $12–29. The risks: buyers may not understand the format, mobile behavior needs testing, and Etsy's accepted digital file types should be confirmed before building (a ZIP or PDF wrapper may be needed).

## Compliance and fees decide which ideas survive

Etsy allows AI-assisted digital products under **"Designed by a seller"**, provided the seller **discloses AI use in the listing description** and the design is the seller's own original work ([Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)). The Seller Handbook cites a custom pet portrait generated from a buyer's request as an acceptable example ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090)), which covers the custom ideas above. The verified tightening is the **June 10, 2025** update. It brought digital downloads into scope, removed "templated design or pattern" as an acceptable basis, and excluded AI prompt bundles and repackaged third-party work ([ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-04-11-etsy-creativity-standards-digital-downloads-template-sellers)). Claims of a January 2026 enforcement wave with about 12,000 removals, and an August 2026 "computerized tools" rule, appear only on secondary blogs and are **unverified** ([Growtsy](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-ai-policy)). Enforcement cuts both ways. Sellers report automated false-positive removals of original work and slow appeals ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/My-listings-continue-to-be-wrongfully-taken-down-by-Etsy-s-AI/m-p/146626966)).

| Checklist item | What to do | Basis |
|---|---|---|

Fees reward the higher price points this report recommends. US sellers pay a **$0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 processing**, plus a reported one-time setup fee of about $15 ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026)). On a $5 printable that is about **$0.93, or 19%**. On a $50 custom order it is about **$5.20, or 10%**, rising to roughly **$12.70 (25%)** if the sale came from an Offsite Ad, which costs 15% for shops under $10k a year (opt-out allowed) and 12%, mandatory, above that ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees)). New shops, digital-only ones included, can face a **payment reserve** ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Payment-reserve/m-p/148383058)), so don't count on December cash arriving immediately. Star Seller for a digital shop depends mostly on **95%+ message response within 24 hours and a 4.8+ rating** ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/how-to-become-etsy-star-seller)), and custom-order work is built around messaging anyway.

## Validate every idea first: the research never saw live Etsy data

The research proxy blocked etsy.com, eRank's help pages and every seller-tool dashboard. **No live listing counts, review counts or sales figures were collected.** Every Etsy number above comes from search-engine snippets of third-party pages, and several conflict. The ranking is a hypothesis to test, not a verdict. Even the tools you'll use are approximate. EverBee claims about 80% estimate accuracy, and Alura calls its data "a reliable indicator rather than exact numbers" ([revenuegeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/everbee)).

Run one validation pass, about 20 minutes per idea, before production. In eRank's Keyword Tool, or EverBee's, pull monthly searches, competing listings and click-through rate for 3–5 long-tail phrases per idea. Seed phrases: "christmas murder mystery game", "custom cross stitch pattern from photo", "family recipe book from handwritten recipes", "custom coloring book from photos", "caregiver binder", "mahjong party invitation", "gothmas", "personalized santa video", "glp-1 tracker". Proceed if the search-to-listing ratio is **above 0.5**, with roughly **500–5,000 monthly searches** and **under 5,000 listings for the exact phrase** ([getbetterlisting](https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/2026-etsy-niche-research-tips); [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/low-competition-etsy-niches)). Then search Etsy directly as a buyer. Count how many of the top 48 results are truly on-target, note their prices and review counts, and look for the weaknesses this report expects (AI mockups, no proofs, slow turnaround). In EverBee, check whether the top three on-target listings show steady monthly sales rather than one-time spikes. For custom ideas, gauge willingness to pay by listing one tier and watching favorites and carts for 7–10 days before building a full pipeline. Any idea that fails these checks drops to the bench. Any bench idea that passes strongly moves up.

Gift buyers are already searching, with "christmas" at #4 and "christmas ornament" at #20 in September ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy/)). Listings need several weeks to collect the engagement signals Etsy search reportedly rewards ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)), so the plan front-loads premade seasonal products and stages custom products behind a tested proof workflow.

## Conclusion

The research turns the usual AI-on-Etsy pitch upside down. AI's advantage is not producing more files faster. That advantage went to everyone at once, and it is why the head categories sell for $1. The lasting advantage is taking on **personalization and assembly work that used to be too labor-intensive to price under $50**: transcribing a grandmother's recipes, charting a pet as a stitch pattern, scripting a party where the suspects are your friends. The parts AI still does badly, like testing a game's logic, stitching a sample or confirming a likeness, are where a solo seller's trust is built. The analog revival actually favors a seller who uses AI honestly to make offline, personal objects.

The bigger uncertainty is not demand but verification. Every Etsy-side number here is secondhand, the policy timeline beyond June 2025 is contested, and the tool licenses keep changing. The practical stance is to run Etsy as an experiment platform. Validate cheaply, launch small, keep process evidence, and let December's conversion data, not this ranking, decide what scales in 2027.
